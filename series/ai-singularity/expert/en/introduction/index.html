Why 2025-2030 is Critical

Moore's Law is dead for traditional CPUs, but it is alive and accelerating for AI. We are witnessing "scaling laws" where performance improves predictably with compute and data. This isn't just a tech upgrade; it's a new industrial revolution.

Between 2025 and 2030, we expect AI to transition from "Chatbots" to "Agents" to "Physical World Interaction". This series maps that journey.

Concept: Emergent Capabilities

In complex systems, "emergence" occurs when the whole is greater than the sum of its parts. In AI, as we scale parameter count and training data, models suddenly acquire skills they were never explicitly taught—like coding, reasoning, or translation. This non-linear capability jump is why forecasts are consistently too conservative.
