The Compute Arms Race

The entire internet infrastructure is being rebuilt from scratch. The shift from CPU-centric to GPU-centric computing is a $1 Trillion opportunity.

$1T Capex Blackwell Era Sovereign AI Power Constraint

The Capex Tsunami

Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon) are engaging in a capital expenditure arms race. They cannot afford to fall behind. This is not just about buying chips; it's about building "AI Factories" — massive data centers designed as a single computer.

The Investment Targets

We break down the "Compute" trade into three layers: The Chip Designers, The Foundries, and The Infrastructure.

Concept: Training vs. Inference

Training is teaching the AI (building the brain). This requires massive clusters of the most powerful GPUs (H100/Blackwell). Inference is using the AI (the brain answering questions). As models get deployed, inference compute will dwarf training compute. This opens the door for specialized, lower-power chips (ASICs) and edge computing.

Risks to Watch

The "Air Pocket" Risk

The biggest risk to the compute trade is a temporary "air pocket" in demand. If AI revenue doesn't materialize fast enough for software companies, they might pause capex for 1-2 quarters. This would cause a 30-40% drawdown in chip stocks. We view this as a buying opportunity, not a thesis killer.
