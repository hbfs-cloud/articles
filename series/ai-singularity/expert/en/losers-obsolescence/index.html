The Losers: Obsolescence Trades

Capitalism is creative destruction. AI is about to destroy a lot of capital. If your business is "middleman arbitrage", your stock is going to zero.

Business Model Extinction Call Centers Translation Legal Research

The "Digital Commodity" Thesis

If the service you sell can be produced by an LLM for $0.01, you no longer have a business. You have a hobby. The market is slow to price this in because these companies still have cash flow today. But the terminal value is zero.

Concept: The Innovator's Dilemma

Incumbents (like Google or Accenture) *can* adopt AI, but it cannibalizes their existing high-margin revenue. A startup (like Perplexity or an AI Agent firm) has no revenue to protect, so they can offer the same service for 90% less. This structural disadvantage kills the giants who refuse to eat their own lunch.
