Democratizing Hedge Fund Strategies

Old algo trading was about speed (HFT). New AI trading is about understanding. An LLM can read every earnings call transcript, every patent filing, and every news article in real-time, synthesize the "mood" of the market, and predict price movements based on unstructured data that traditional quants couldn't touch.
