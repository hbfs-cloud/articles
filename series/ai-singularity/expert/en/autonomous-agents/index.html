Autonomous AI Agents

The era of "Chat" is ending. The era of "Do" is beginning. Software is eating software.

Agentic Frameworks Enterprise Automation Margin Expansion BPO Disruption

From Chatbots to Agents

A chatbot (ChatGPT) answers your question. An Autonomous Agent takes a goal ("Book me a flight to London under $600") and executes it (searches, compares, books, adds to calendar, emails receipt). This shift from passive information retrieval to active task execution is the "Killer App" for AI in the enterprise.

Concept: Agentic Loops

Agents operate in a loop: Observe -> Reason -> Act -> Reflect. Unlike traditional software which follows a rigid script (if this, then that), agents can adapt to errors. If a flight booking fails, the agent reads the error, tries a different airline, or alerts the user—just like a human assistant would.
