Autonomous Driving

The technology is finally catching up to the hype. Robotaxis will collapse the cost of transport by 80%.

Level 4 Autonomy Neural Planner Cost Collapse Insurance Crisis

The Economic Case

Today, an Uber ride costs ~$2.50 per mile. 70% of that goes to the driver. Remove the driver, electricify the fleet, and maximize utilization, and the cost drops to ~$0.30 per mile. This makes transport cheaper than owning a car.

Concept: End-to-End Neural Nets

Old self-driving cars (like early Waymo) used heuristic rules ("if red light, stop"). The new generation (Tesla FSD v12+) uses End-to-End Neural Networks. The car watches millions of hours of human driving video and learns to drive by imitation, without a single line of C++ code telling it what a stop sign is. This is the breakthrough that enables scale.
