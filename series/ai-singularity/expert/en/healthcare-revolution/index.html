Healthcare Revolution

We have decoded the source code of life. AI is transforming biology from a "wet science" of trial and error into a computable engineering problem.

Digital Biology AlphaFold Precision Med Fast Trials

The "TechBio" Paradigm Shift

Traditionally, discovering a new drug takes 10-12 years and costs $2.5 billion, with a 90% failure rate. AI changes the math. By simulating molecular interactions in silico (on a computer) before testing in vivo, we can screen billions of compounds in weeks, not years.

Concept: The AlphaFold Moment

Proteins are the building blocks of life. Their function is determined by their 3D shape. For 50 years, predicting this shape from a DNA sequence was an unsolved grand challenge. In 2020, Google DeepMind's AlphaFold solved it. We now have the 3D structure of nearly every protein known to science. This is the "Google Maps" for biology.
