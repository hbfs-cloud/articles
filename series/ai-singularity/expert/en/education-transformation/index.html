Education Transformation

The "Factory Model" of education is dead. AI enables the holy grail of pedagogy: Personalized, 1-on-1 tutoring for everyone, at zero marginal cost.

AI Tutors Reskilling Personalized University Crisis

The 2 Sigma Problem: Solved

Research shows that students with a 1-on-1 tutor perform "two sigmas" (98th percentile) better than students in a classroom. Until now, tutoring was too expensive to scale. AI changes this. An AI tutor can adapt to a student's pace, learning style, and interests instantly.

Concept: Adaptive Learning Paths

A textbook is static. Chapter 1 is always Chapter 1. An AI learning path is dynamic. If a student struggles with quadratic equations because they forgot algebra, the AI detects the gap and inserts a 5-minute algebra refresher before moving on. It's a curriculum that rewrites itself in real-time.
