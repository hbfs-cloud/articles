The General Purpose Robot

For 50 years, robots were blind, dumb machines bolted to the floor doing one task repeatedly. "Physical AI" gives them eyes (vision transformers) and a brain (LLMs). The goal is a General Purpose Humanoid that can work in any factory designed for humans.

Concept: Sim-to-Real Transfer

Training a robot in the real world is slow and dangerous. Instead, we train them in a physics-accurate simulation (like NVIDIA Omniverse) where they can practice millions of years of experience in a day. We then transfer this "muscle memory" to the physical robot. This is why progress is accelerating so fast.
