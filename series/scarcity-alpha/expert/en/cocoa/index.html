Shrinkflation Explained

When input costs (cocoa) triple, companies like Hershey and Mondelez can't triple the price of a KitKat without losing customers. Instead, they reduce the size of the bar by 10% and keep the price the same. Or they change the recipe to include more sugar/fillers and less cocoa butter. This "shrinkflation" is the first line of defense for margins.
