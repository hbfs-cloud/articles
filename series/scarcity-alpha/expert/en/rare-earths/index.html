Not Rare, But Difficult

Rare Earth elements (like Neodymium) are actually quite common in the earth's crust. The problem is they are rarely found in concentrated deposits, and separating them from the ore is a toxic, chemical-intensive process. China was willing to tolerate the pollution for decades to gain market share. The West wasn't. Now, the cost of that outsourcing is becoming clear.
