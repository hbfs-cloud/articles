The PFAS Opportunity

The EPA has set strict limits on PFAS ("forever chemicals") in drinking water. Utilities across the US must now invest billions in filtration systems. This is a mandatory capex cycle. Companies like Xylem and Evoqua (acquired by Xylem) provide the filtration tech. It's a regulatory tailwind that exists regardless of the economy.
