Power > Space

In data center real estate, square footage doesn't matter. Megawatts matter. A 100,000 sq ft warehouse with 5MW of power is worth less than a 50,000 sq ft facility with 50MW. The constraint is the grid connection, not the building. DLR and EQIX are essentially "power arbitrage" companies.
