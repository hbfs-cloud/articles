The Arbitrage

US gas is cheap ($2-3/MMBtu) because of abundant shale supply. European/Asian gas is expensive ($10-15/MMBtu). Companies like Cheniere exist to exploit this arbitrage by liquefying the gas (cooling it to -160°C) and shipping it. As long as the arbitrage window is open (price spread > shipping/liquefaction cost), LNG is a money printer.
