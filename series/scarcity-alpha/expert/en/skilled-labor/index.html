The Automation Paradox

We often hear that robots will take jobs. In construction, robots are saving jobs by filling the gaps. Companies like AAON (HVAC) are automating their factories not to fire people, but because they simply cannot find enough welders. Investing in companies that enable automation (or provide equipment to leverage scarce humans) is the play.
