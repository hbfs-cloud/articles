Deep Dive: What is CoWoS?

Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate (CoWoS) is an advanced packaging technology by TSMC. It allows stacking memory (HBM) and logic (GPU) side-by-side on a silicon interposer. Why it matters: You can print the GPU chips, but if you can't package them with CoWoS, they are useless for AI. TSMC's CoWoS capacity is the true bottleneck of the entire AI industry.
