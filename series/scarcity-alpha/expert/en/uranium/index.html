Why AI needs Nuclear

AI training clusters run 24/7 at 100% load. Solar and wind are intermittent (intermittency is fatal for training runs). Batteries are too expensive for week-long backups. Natural gas has carbon emissions that tech companies have pledged to eliminate. Nuclear is the only source that is 24/7, carbon-free, and scalable. That's why Microsoft is restarting Three Mile Island.
