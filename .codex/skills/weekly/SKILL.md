# Weekly

Read `.claude/commands/weekly.md`, `weekly/CLAUDE.md`, and `.claude/skills/source-policy.md`. Execute the
runbook exactly, including its date contract, `validate-workflows.js --workflow weekly`, evidence gates
and same-snapshot reviews. External publication occurs only when authorized by the invocation.

Preserve the established rich Weekly information design: a decision summary first, followed by interpreted
ECharts for cross-asset context, event intensity, the systemic earnings catalyst and blast radius, rotation,
risk and allocation. Never let a pure implied-move ranking exclude a systemically important large-cap event;
validate that the options expiration is after the event before using the move.
