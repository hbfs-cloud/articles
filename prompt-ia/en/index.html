#1

              
Initial Screening

            

            Stock-Picking
            
          

          

            
Find candidates based on your fundamental criteria. Replace [SECTOR] with your target.

            
I'm looking for stocks in the [SECTOR] sector with: a P/E < 20, revenue growth > 10%/year over 3 years, net debt/EBITDA < 2. Give me 10 candidates with their key metrics. For each one, indicate a major risk I might overlook. Also expand to adjacent sectors I may not have considered.

            
 Source: Bien Débuter en Bourse

          

        


        
        

          

            

              
#2

              
Adversarial Analysis ⭐

            

            Most important
            
          

          

            
The most important prompt. Forces the AI to play devil's advocate to identify risks you're overlooking.

            
I want to buy [TICKER] at [PRICE]. Play the role of an aggressive bear analyst: give me 7 reasons why this trade could fail. Include macro, sector, valuation risks, and specific risks that bulls often ignore. Don't be polite, be honest.

            
 Source: Bien Débuter en Bourse

          

        


        
        

          

            

              
#3

              
Detailed Comparison

            

            Stock-Picking
            
          

          

            
Compare multiple tickers across all axes (valuation, growth, quality, momentum).

            
Compare [TICKER A] vs [TICKER B] vs [TICKER C] on these criteria: valuation (P/E, EV/EBITDA, PEG), growth (revenue, EPS), quality (net margin, ROE, debt), momentum (6-month performance, RSI). Create a table and conclude with a reasoned ranking. Also mention competitors I may have overlooked.

            
 Source: Bien Débuter en Bourse

          

        


        
        

          

            

              
#4

              
Thematic Expansion

            

            Stock-Picking
            
          

          

            
Explore beyond the obvious names: picks & shovels, indirect beneficiaries, thematic ETFs.

            
I'm interested in [THEME/SECTOR]. Beyond the obvious names, what are the lesser-known players, indirect beneficiaries (picks & shovels), and systemic risks that retail investors overlook? Also cover thematic ETFs and proxies for gaining exposure to this trend.

            
 Source: Bien Débuter en Bourse

          

        


        
        

          

            

              
#5

              
Portfolio Audit

            

            Portfolio
            
          

          

            
Full diagnostic of your portfolio: concentration, correlations, factors, crash risk. Do it once a week.

            
Here is my portfolio: [LIST OF POSITIONS + % ALLOCATION]. Analyze it like a fund manager: 1) Sector and geographic concentration 2) Likely correlations between positions 3) Factor exposure (growth, value, momentum, quality) 4) Drawdown risk in a crash (-20% on the S&P 500) 5) What's missing to improve the risk/reward. Be critical.

            
 Source: Construction de Portefeuille

DCA Optimization

            

            Portfolio
            
          

          

            
Adapt your monthly investment to market conditions (ATH, correction, bear market).

            
I invest $[AMOUNT]/month via DCA. My target allocation: [BREAKDOWN]. The market is currently [CONTEXT: all-time high / -10% correction / bear market]. Should I: a) maintain my standard allocation, b) overweight certain assets, c) temporarily increase cash? Back your argument with historical data on DCA during similar periods.

#8

              
Macro Stress-Test

            

            Portfolio
            
          

          

            
Simulate the impact of 4 catastrophe scenarios on your portfolio. Do it every quarter.

            
Simulate the impact on my portfolio [LIST OF POSITIONS] of these 4 scenarios: 1) US recession with S&P -30% 2) Persistent inflation at 6%+ with hawkish Fed 3) Major geopolitical crisis (Taiwan/NATO) 4) Tech crash (-40% on the Nasdaq). For each scenario, estimate the loss in % and identify the most vulnerable positions.

#11

              
Earnings Analysis

            

            Earnings
            
          

          

            
Analyze a company's quarterly results in 5 key points.

            
Analyze the quarterly results of [TICKER] ([QUARTER]).

1. Summary in 5 bullet points (EPS, revenue, margins, guidance, surprise)
2. Compare with analyst consensus
3. Identify the 3 major risks mentioned in the call
4. Score management sentiment (1 to 10)
5. Give a verdict: buy, hold, or sell

Include the call transcript or 10-K link if available.

 Key tip

        
AI is an assistant, not an oracle. Use the generated prompt as a starting point, then verify the data on primary sources (Yahoo Finance, SEC EDGAR, TradingView). Our series IA et Bourse details the 5 pitfalls to avoid.

      

    


    

      
 Known limitations

      
    
        
  • Hallucinations — AIs can make up numbers. The prompt includes anti-hallucination rules, but always verify.
    • 
        
  • Stale data — Only ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and Gemini have web access. Claude and DeepSeek work with their internal knowledge.
    • 
        
  • Confirmation bias — If you provide a strong thesis, the AI may confirm it instead of challenging it. Vary your intents (buy vs sell).
    • 
        
  • Length — In "Detailed" format, the response may exceed the AI's limit. It will stop and ask you to type "Continue".
