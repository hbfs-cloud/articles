# EON Resources: we were right for the wrong reason

*Updated on 4 August closes. Grade held at D.*

In June we put a D on EON Resources and named the danger: dilution. A $150m equity facility sat open above a company worth $28m, and those facilities usually end one way — the share count climbs, the price falls, and the two feed each other.

Six weeks later the stock is $0.4942, down 13.2% from the 9 June close of $0.569. Anyone who bought on our April note at $0.83 is down 40.5%.

No further dilution happened. Our June note had already recorded 11%; since then the share count has not moved.

## Three things that went against us

**The share count did not move.** 49,968,344 shares outstanding. That is the same number as June. Not approximately — exactly.

**The short sellers left.** In June, 10.84% of the tradeable shares were sold short. Today it is 7.6%, or 3,182,475 shares.

**Management bought with their own money.** Between 30 June and 26 November 2025 the filings show **fifteen open-market purchases and no sales at all**: 1,107,188 shares for $412,287, at an average of $0.3724 against today's $0.4942. That is 2.65% of the tradeable float, and all five took part — chief executive Dante Caravaggio, chief financial officer Mitchell Trotter, VP of finance Mark Williams, and directors Joseph Salvucci Sr and Jr.

One thing to hold against it. On 16 February 2026 the same five exercised options at a zero strike, adding 211,667 shares without spending anything. Some data services count those as insider buying. They are not, and we separate them out.

## But somebody who knew more was selling

Pogo Royalty holds more than 10% of the company. It is also the creditor behind the $15m note EON issued when it bought the field — so it reads this balance sheet as a lender, not just as a shareholder.

Between September 2024 and January 2025 it sold **1,510,000 shares for $2.37m**, at an average of $1.5682. That is roughly three times today's price. It now holds 500,000.

Management was buying at $0.37. The party owed $15m by the company was leaving at $1.57. When those two disagree, the creditor usually has the better information.

## What is actually wrong

The company stopped filing.

EON has sent nothing to the SEC since **28 April**. No annual report, no quarterly report, no form of any kind.

The 2025 annual report was due on 15 April. It is 112 days late. And because it is outstanding, the two quarterly reports for 2026 cannot be filed either.

Add that up. The most recent financial statement any outsider can read covers the three months that ended in **September 2025**. Everything since is unknown.

In February the company filed a form declaring its own earlier accounts unreliable. That restatement is still unresolved, and it is the stated reason the audit has not closed.

## What it owes

Roughly **$40m**, against $875,604 of cash and a $24.7m market value.

The senior facility is a reserve-based loan from First International Bank & Trust, originally $28m when the field was bought in November 2023. It stood at **$21.4m on 30 June 2025**. On top sits the Pogo seller note: **$15m** of principal plus around **$4m** of accrued interest.

Put plainly, the creditors own most of this enterprise and the shareholders own the sliver left over.

Two corrections to our own record while we are here. Our June note published $44.1m and attributed it to a Form 10-K/A. No such filing exists for this company — the citation was wrong, even though the magnitude was about right. And a market data field we could have leaned on carries $5.39m, which is not consistent with the loan balances in the filings. We are not using it.

## The date that matters

On 16 April, NYSE Regulation notified EON it was out of compliance with the rules for staying listed. The company has until **15 October 2026** to file the annual report. That is 71 days away.

Miss it, and the exchange may grant another six months, or begin removing the stock. Both are allowed. Neither is promised. For now EONR still trades normally.

## Meanwhile

Since late June the company has published a drilling programme update, a growth and spending plan running to 2030, and the results of a rock study on one of its fields.

A five-year spending plan from a company that cannot produce last year's audited accounts is not a plan. Every operational figure in those announcements is management describing itself, with nobody checking.

## Where it trades

The stock sits below all three of its main moving averages, stacked bearishly: the 20-day at $0.5177, the 50-day at $0.5523, the 200-day at $0.5614. Momentum is still deteriorating.

The working floor is **$0.43**, the low of 24 June and again of 2 July, held both times. Below it, $0.42, $0.39, $0.36, then the 52-week low at $0.267. Overhead, $0.62 is the first real resistance. Daily range is 8.3% of the price.

One more correction. The previous version of this page listed resistance at $4.00 and $3.30 on a stock trading under a dollar. That was a scale error from a stale source. Fixed.

## Still D

The grade holds, but almost nothing behind it is the same.

The stated book value is $1.352 a share against a $0.4942 quote — a 63% discount. That reads like an argument to buy until you notice the discount is calculated from the one number nobody has audited.

There is also a timing detail worth sitting with. The insider buying stopped on 26 November. The company declared its own accounts unreliable in February. Nobody at the top has bought a share on the open market since — and people buying at $0.3724 last autumn were not vouching for numbers that had not yet been called into question.

One event changes this: the 2025 annual report landing before 15 October. It would resolve the listing problem and put a signed number on the $40m we have had to assemble from year-old filings.

Two things would tell you we were early rather than wrong: a share count above 49,968,344, or a new prospectus supplement. Either means the facility is finally being drawn.

*Analysis, not advice. This is a distressed micro-cap with no analyst coverage. Size accordingly, or skip it.*
