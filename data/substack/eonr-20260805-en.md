In June we put a D on EON Resources and said the danger was dilution. A $150m financing facility sat open above a company worth $28m, and those facilities usually end one way: the share count climbs, the price falls, and the two feed each other.

Six weeks later the stock is at $0.4942, down another 12.9%. Anyone who bought on our April note at $0.83 is down 40.4%.

And the dilution never came.

## What actually happened

Shares outstanding: 49,968,344. That is the same number as in June. Not roughly the same. The same.

Short interest went the other way too. In June, 10.84% of the tradeable shares were sold short. Today it is 6.76%, about 3.2 million shares. The people betting against it have mostly left.

So the specific machine we warned about did not run. The facility is still there on paper. It has not been used to print stock this quarter.

We were right about the direction and wrong about the reason. That distinction matters, because the real reason is worse.

## The company stopped filing

EON has not sent anything to the SEC since 28 April. Not a quarterly report, not an annual report, not a single form of any kind. Ninety-nine days of silence.

The annual report for 2025 was due on 15 April. It is 112 days late.

Because that annual report is missing, the two quarterly reports for 2026 cannot be filed either. Add it up: the most recent financial statement any outsider can read covers the three months that ended in **September 2025**. Everything after that is unknown.

In February the company filed a form saying its own earlier accounts could not be relied on. That restatement is still not resolved, and it is the stated reason the audit has not closed.

## There is a date on this

On 16 April, NYSE Regulation told EON it was out of compliance with the rules for staying listed. The company has until **15 October 2026** to file the annual report. That is 71 days away.

If it misses that date, the exchange may grant another six months, or it may start removing the stock from the market. Both are allowed. Neither is promised.

## The part that is hard to read charitably

While the audit sits unfinished, the company has kept issuing news.

On 26 June, an update on its 2026 drilling programme. On 30 June, a growth and spending plan running to 2030. On 27 July, the results of a rock study on one of its fields.

A five-year spending plan from a company that cannot produce last year's audited accounts is not a plan. It is a press release. Every operational figure in those announcements is management describing itself, with nobody checking.

## What the cheapness is worth

The stated book value is $1.352 a share. The stock trades at $0.4942. That is a 63% discount, wider than the 54% we noted in June.

That looks like an argument to buy. It is not, and the reason is simple: the number that produces the discount is exactly the number nobody has audited. You cannot buy a dollar for thirty-seven cents if no accountant will confirm the dollar is there.

## Where it trades

The stock sits under all three of its main moving averages, and they are stacked in the bearish order: the 20-day at $0.5177, the 50-day at $0.5523, the 200-day at $0.5614.

The floor that matters is **$0.43**. It was the low on 24 June and again on 2 July, and it held both times. Below that, the next reference is the 52-week low at $0.267. Overhead, $0.575 and then $0.62 have capped every bounce since mid-July.

Daily range is about 8.3% of the price, so a single session can cover a fifth of the distance down to that floor.

One correction while we are here. The previous version of this page listed resistance at $4.00 and $3.30 on a stock trading under a dollar. That was a scale error on our side, carried over from a stale data source. The levels above are taken from daily bars.

## Verdict: still D

The grade does not move, but the case behind it has been rewritten.

There is one event that changes this, and only one: the 2025 annual report landing before 15 October. If it arrives, the listing problem goes away and the balance sheet becomes something a person can actually analyse. Until then there is nothing here to underwrite, however cheap the quote looks.

Watch instead for a share count above 49,968,344. That would mean the facility is finally being drawn, and that the June thesis was early rather than wrong.
