Portfolio Replication

Free JSON endpoints to replicate the scanner portfolio. Updated every trading session (~23:00 UTC weekdays).

Live No auth 6 endpoints Nightly refresh

Quick Start

1

Fetch signals

Call /portfolio/v1/signals.json after each evening scan to get the latest picks with entry, stop, and target levels.

2

Track positions

Use /portfolio/v1/positions.json to see current open positions with live P&L and days remaining.

3

Replicate

Equal-weight allocation (25% per position, 4 slots). Follow entry range, set stop and TP. Close after 5 trading days max.
