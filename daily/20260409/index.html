What Just Happened — Wednesday's Hawkish Slap

Wednesday's session was defined by a single 6-minute window at 18:00 GMT: the release of the March FOMC minutes. Markets entered flat after Tuesday's digestion of the Iran de-escalation headline. Then the minutes landed, and they were notably more hawkish than Powell's press conference tone. Several participants — per the text — explicitly discussed the risk that "a premature easing could undermine the Committee's credibility should services inflation fail to moderate further in coming months." The word "patient" appeared 11 times, "progress" only 4. Translation: the committee is not eager to cut in June.

The knee-jerk was clean: 2Y UST ripped +8bp to 4.48%, 10Y +6bp to 4.41%, DXY +0.6%, SPY −0.9% in 20 minutes. The rally from Monday's catch-up squeeze gave back roughly 45% of its gains, landing SPY right on the 20-DMA at 656. QQQ took the brunt as duration-sensitive megacaps led the fade: NVDA −1.8%, MSFT −1.3%, META −1.5%. Breadth flipped to 62% decliners, and the VIX pushed back above 22.

Why minutes matter more than the statement. The FOMC statement is a consensus document, deliberately vague. The minutes reveal the distribution of views. When hawks dominate the "risk assessment" paragraphs — as they did Wednesday — the market reprices the probability of a later, shallower cutting cycle even if the median dot hasn't moved. Today's CPI is the referendum: soft and the minutes become irrelevant; hot and they become prophecy.

Formation — How to Trade a CPI Release Without Getting Chopped

The 3-Minute Rule and the 9-Minute Reversal

CPI prints at 12:30 GMT sharp. The first 3 minutes are pure algorithmic reaction: futures move on headline and core y/y, then options dealers re-hedge, then CTAs recalibrate. If you're trading the print, you're in the machines' arena — probability-weighted bad idea for discretionary traders.

The 3-minute rule. Wait at least 3 minutes after the release before placing any directional trade. Let the first flush of flow clear. Check: is SPY above or below the pre-release level after 3 minutes? That's your trend tell.

The 9-minute reversal window. Historically, 58% of CPI-day sessions see a reversal between minutes 6 and 12 after the print as the initial move exhausts. If the print is in line (±0.1%) and the market initially overreacts, fade the move once the 5-minute candle closes back inside the pre-release range. Target 50–60% retracement of the initial move. Tight stop: the post-release high/low + 1 tick.

Key levels for today's trade. SPY pre-release zone: 655–657. A break below 652 after CPI opens the gap fill at 648. A push above 660 invalidates the bearish thesis and targets 665. For QQQ, watch 582 (support) and 590 (resistance). BTC trades in sympathy but with a 15-minute lag — typically catches up to equities within half an hour.

Sizing rule. Cut your normal position size by 50% on CPI days. The implied move is 0.9% on SPY today — expect 1.5× that in practice. The same R/R with half the size keeps you in the game for tomorrow's PPI and Friday's bank earnings.
