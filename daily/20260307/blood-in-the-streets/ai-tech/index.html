Quantum Computing Investing: Separating Hype from Reality

The Quantum Promise

Quantum computers can solve problems impossible for classical computers: drug discovery, materials science, cryptography, optimization. The TAM is potentially $1T+. But timelines are highly uncertain. Most experts say 5-10 years for commercial quantum advantage.

Three Approaches

RGTI uses superconducting qubits (like Google, IBM). IONQ uses trapped ions (highest fidelity). QBTS uses quantum annealing (most commercially mature). Each has trade-offs. There is no clear winner yet, which is why diversification across approaches makes sense.

The Revenue Reality Check

Combined revenue of RGTI + QBTS + IONQ is under $100M. Combined market cap is $25B. That is a massive gap. These stocks trade on potential, not fundamentals. Price them accordingly: small positions, long time horizons, and willingness to lose 100%.

How to Play It Safely

Invest the majority in AI companies with real revenue (CRDO, ESTC, BRZE). Allocate small satellite positions (0.3-0.5% each) to quantum names for asymmetric upside. Never let quantum positions exceed 2% of total portfolio. These are lottery tickets with better odds.
