Business Overview

Veeco Instruments designs and manufactures advanced thin-film process equipment used in semiconductor fabrication, compound semiconductor manufacturing, and data storage applications. The company's laser annealing systems are essential for next-generation chip miniaturization below 3nm nodes.

Key products include laser spike annealing (LSA) systems for advanced logic chips, ion beam systems for EUV mask repair, and MOCVD systems for LED and power electronics. With AI driving unprecedented demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced packaging, Veeco sits at a critical junction in the semiconductor supply chain. The company serves foundries (TSMC, Samsung), memory makers (SK Hynix, Micron), and IDMs worldwide.
