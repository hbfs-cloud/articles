Business Overview

General Dynamics is one of the world's largest defense contractors, operating across four segments: Aerospace (Gulfstream business jets), Marine Systems (Columbia- and Virginia-class submarines, surface combatants), Combat Systems (Abrams tanks, Stryker and wheeled vehicles, munitions) and Technologies (GDIT IT services + Mission Systems). Roughly two-thirds of revenue is tied to long-cycle US and allied government programs, giving the business a backlog-driven, recurring-revenue character that smooths economic cycles StockAnalysis· jun 2026.

The Q1 2026 print captured the franchise at full stride: revenue of $13.5B (+10.3% YoY) with Marine Systems up 21% on submarine production and Aerospace delivering a record 38 first-quarter Gulfstream jets at a 15% margin GD IR· apr 2026. The structural moat — decades-long platform programs, deep government relationships and a $188B total estimated contract value — is exactly what makes GD a compounder rather than a cyclical trade.
