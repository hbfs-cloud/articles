On August 19, 2026, Trio-Tech International (the “ Company ”) issued a press release announcing the transfer of the listing of its common stock, no par value (“ Common Stock ”) from the the New York Stock Exchange American (the “NYSE”) to the Nasdaq Global Market of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“ NASDAQ ”) (the “ Exchange Transfer ”).
