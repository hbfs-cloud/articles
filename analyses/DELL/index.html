Business Overview

Dell Technologies is one of the world's largest hardware and IT infrastructure companies, organized around two pillars. The Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) sells servers, storage and networking — and is now the epicentre of Dell's AI story, shipping high-density GPU servers (NVIDIA-based) to hyperscalers and enterprises building AI factories. The Client Solutions Group (CSG) covers commercial and consumer PCs, a mature, cash-generative but cyclical franchise. Trailing-twelve-month revenue is $134.0B, growing +87% YoY as AI server orders scale Finviz· live.

The moat is scale, supply-chain mastery and enterprise relationships: Dell can configure, finance and service AI infrastructure at a volume few peers match. The trade-off is mix — servers carry thinner margins than software, so blended operating margin sits near 8.3%. The investment thesis is that AI backlog converts into durable ISG revenue while the buyback shrinks the share count and lifts per-share earnings. The stock has rallied from a 52-week low of $110 to $396, a ~3.6x move, but still trades below its $469 high Yahoo Finance· live.
