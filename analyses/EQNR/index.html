Equinor is Norway's energy powerhouse — an integrated oil, gas and renewables company backed by the Norwegian state (67% ownership). With $106B in revenue, record production of 2.14M bpd, and a 3.6% dividend yield supported by aggressive buybacks, EQNR is a textbook defensive energy play. The catch? At $41.53, it trades 35% above the analyst consensus target of $30.88 and RSI sits at 77 (overbought). Strong fundamentals, but timing matters.

Bull Case

  • Record production 2.14M bpd — Johan Castberg & Bacalhau ramping
  • Negative beta (-0.23) — portfolio diversifier uncorrelated to SPY/QQQ
  • No dilution risk — share count declining 7-8%/year
