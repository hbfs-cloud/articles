Business Overview

Intuitive Machines (LUNR) began as a lunar-lander developer, it put the first US commercial spacecraft on the Moon, and is now repositioning as a broader space-infrastructure prime. Its work spans NASA's Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) missions, lunar data relay and communications, orbital services, and, increasingly, national-security and commercial satellite programs.
