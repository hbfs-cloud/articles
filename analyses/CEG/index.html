Verdict Express

B Neutral Moderate confidence

Constellation offers the cleanest fundamental exposure to rising U.S. power demand in this batch because it already owns a large operating fleet and produces earnings today. Q2 2026 GAAP EPS was $1.42 versus $2.67, but non-GAAP adjusted operating EPS rose to $2.55 from $1.91. Management raised FY2026 adjusted operating EPS guidance to $11.50-$12.50 after incorporating Calpine and favorable market and portfolio conditions, partly offset by nuclear outages. The combined fleet is roughly 55 GW, serves about 2.5 million retail accounts and added 920 MW of long-term clean-power agreements. Crane's targeted 2027 restart adds a discrete execution catalyst rather than distant pipeline rhetoric. The counterweight is leverage and integration: point-in-time debt is about $24.7 billion, Calpine increases gas and market exposure, and the shares trade near 15.5x EV/EBITDA. Shelf registrations provide financing capacity but are not issuance. The B grade is defensible for operating quality and scale; timing remains only a watch until $284.09 activates.

Business Overview

Constellation owns and operates nuclear, gas and other generation while selling electricity and energy products to wholesale and retail customers. Revenue and earnings depend on generation output, realized power prices, hedges, fuel and outage costs, retail margins and fleet availability.

The AI transmission channel is electricity itself: datacenters require reliable around-the-clock supply, which supports long-term power agreements, capacity prices and nuclear life-extension or restart economics. Constellation benefits when customers contract directly for clean generation rather than waiting for new transmission and generation.

The model breaks through extended nuclear outages, adverse regulation, commodity hedges, weak capacity pricing, Calpine integration failures or excessive leverage. AI demand does not eliminate operational risk: each unavailable reactor removes high-margin output, while the larger gas fleet raises fuel and emissions exposure. Shelf registrations may facilitate financing but do not prove securities were issued. Investors should therefore separate realized generation margins from demand headlines and monitor outage days, hedge coverage, acquisition leverage and customer contract duration every quarter.

Dilution Risk

Constellation's June filing shows roughly 355M period-end common shares and 360M Q2 diluted weighted-average shares, a five-million-share difference that reflects the earnings denominator and potential common equivalents rather than a new five-million-share financing. The point-in-time structure combines approximately $100B of equity value with $24.7B of debt after the Calpine expansion. The January and February 2026 S-3ASR filings are automatic shelf capacity, not completed equity issuance. Acquisition financing, operating-company debt, nuclear obligations and integration requirements are the economically important claims. Capital allocation must balance deleveraging, fleet investment and shareholder returns.
