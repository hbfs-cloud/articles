Business Overview

Snowflake charges primarily for product consumption across data warehousing, engineering, sharing, applications and governance. Product revenue moves with compute and storage usage rather than contracted seats. RPO supplies visibility, while net revenue retention shows whether existing customers expand workloads after optimization.

Cortex AI, Snowpark and native applications connect AI demand to Snowflake credits. The financial transmission occurs when governed enterprise data stays inside Snowflake and production agents repeatedly invoke models, queries and pipelines. A proof of concept can raise activity without producing sustained consumption or protecting product gross margin.

The failure path is renewed optimization, migration toward hyperscaler-native services, or slow RPO conversion. Cloud expense, stock compensation and convertible dilution can keep per-share economics behind product growth. September 2 results must refresh product guidance, NRR and cash-margin assumptions before the displayed setup can be used.

The decisive quarterly bridge runs from signed commitments to consumed credits. If RPO expands while product growth or NRR fades, the backlog is lengthening rather than strengthening, and the current premium has no operating support.
