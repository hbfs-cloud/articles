Business Overview

American Airlines is one of the world's largest airlines, operating a fleet of ~900 mainline aircraft serving 350+ destinations across 60+ countries. The company generates $56B in annual revenue from passenger travel, cargo, and its AAdvantage loyalty program.

AAL is executing a multi-year deleveraging plan to reduce its pandemic-era debt pile of $34.9B. The company has been actively refinancing at lower rates as credit agencies upgrade its outlook. Summer 2026 bookings are at record levels, driven by pent-up international travel demand and strong US domestic leisure spending. At 6.3x forward P/E, the stock is priced for distress despite improving fundamentals.
