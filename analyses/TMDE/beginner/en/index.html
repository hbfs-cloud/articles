Red Flags: The Volume Mystery

On March 3rd, the stock traded **27 million shares**. But there are only **3.5 million shares** available to the public.

What this means:

  • The entire available supply changed hands **nearly 8 times** in one day.
  • This is a classic sign of **coordinated retail trading** (Discord servers telling everyone to buy at once) and high-speed bots trying to beat them to the trade.
  • The "Dump": Just like we saw on March 3rd, the price hit $4.54 and then crashed back to $3.50 in minutes. This happens when the "group leaders" sell their shares to late-comers.
