Quick Summary

TMDE (TMD Energy) is a small energy company from Malaysia that has suddenly become a "hot" speculative play on the US market. While its business is real (moving fuel for ships), its stock price is currently being driven by extreme social media hype and geopolitical rumors.

Beginner Warning: This is a "Penny Stock." It is highly volatile and carries a risk of 100% loss. The recent activity shows signs of coordinated trading by online groups.

Red Flags: Is the Price Being Manipulated?

On March 3rd, something very strange happened: **27 million shares** were traded, even though only **3.5 million shares** are available to the public.

Why this is suspicious:

  • Turnover: The entire supply of the stock was bought and sold 8 times in ONE day.
  • The "Pump": Between 2:30 PM and 3:15 PM, the price skyrocketed to $4.54 on massive volume.
  • The "Dump": Just an hour later, the price crashed back down to $3.50. This pattern often happens when a group (like a Discord server) coordinates a mass buy to trick others into buying high.
