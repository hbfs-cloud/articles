Understanding "Turnover"

When you see 27 million shares traded for a 3.5 million float, it means the entire available supply of the stock changed hands nearly 8 times in a single day. This is characteristic of a high-velocity momentum pump, often driven by day-trading communities and algorithmic bots reacting to news.
