Business Overview

SentinelOne sells endpoint, cloud, identity and data security subscriptions through Singularity. Revenue follows new customers, ARR expansion and additional modules. Customers above $100,000 ARR indicate whether the company is moving from smaller endpoint deployments into broader enterprise contracts.

Purple AI can monetize automation by reducing investigation time, correlating security data and guiding response. Revenue appears through paid module attachment, higher ARR and retention, not chatbot usage. Inference and data costs must remain below incremental subscription value for margin to improve.

The franchise can break if Microsoft bundles security, CrowdStrike wins consolidation budgets, or SentinelOne overspends to defend growth. No debt and ample liquidity buy time, but negative 31% GAAP operating margin means equity compensation and losses still transfer value away from existing shares.

The path to durable profitability requires revenue and ARR to outgrow sales expense, cloud-processing costs and compensation simultaneously. A positive adjusted margin is insufficient when the reported loss remains large and enterprise-customer additions trail total ARR growth.
