Robinhood Q3 Preview: Could Record Revenue Help Turn Shares Around?
Robinhood Markets (NASDAQ:HOOD) looks to win investors back with positive updates when the company reports third-quarter financial results Tuesday after market close.
Robinhood Q3 Earnings Estimates
Analysts expect Robinhood to report third-quarter revenue of $1.42 billion, up from $1.27 billion a year earlier, according to data from Benzinga Pro.
The company has beaten analyst estimates for revenue in seven of the last 10 quarters, including the most recently reported second quarter.
Revenue at that level would be a company record, topping the $1.31 billion reported in the second quarter.
Analysts expect Robinhood to report third-quarter earnings of 56 cents per share, down from 61 cents per share a year earlier.
The company has beaten analyst estimates for earnings per share in eight of the last 10 quarters, including the most recently reported second quarter.
What Analysts Are Saying
Strong product diversification and a large user base led to a recent Outperform initiation from Evercore ISI analyst Adam Frisch.
Frisch, who gave shares a price target of $130, said Robinhood has a "robust user and deposit base that's rapidly scaling."
Here are other recent analyst ratings for Robinhood and their price targets:
Rosenblatt: Initiated with Buy rating, with price target of $135
Needham: Maintained Buy rating, raised price target from $123 to $126
JPMorgan: Maintained Neutral rating, raised price target from $103 to $115
Wells Fargo: Maintained Equal-Weight rating, lowered price target from $125 to $120
Key Items to Watch
Robinhood stock recently fell after reporting August monthly metrics. While some figures, such as funded customers, platform assets and net deposits, showed strong growth, user activity and prediction market volumes were less encouraging for investors.
The company said equity trading volume in August rose 1% from July, while options contracts traded fell 10%.
