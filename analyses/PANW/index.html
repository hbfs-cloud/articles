Business Overview

Palo Alto Networks is the world's largest pure-play cybersecurity vendor, built around three platforms: Strata (network security / next-gen firewalls), Prisma (cloud and SASE security), and Cortex (AI-driven security operations and SIEM/XDR). Its "platformization" strategy bundles these into unified, multi-year contracts — driving Next-Generation Security ARR higher while raising switching costs and net retention across an enterprise installed base.Finviz· live

The model is heavily subscription/SaaS, producing recurring revenue, ~72% gross margins and durable free-cash-flow margins. Demand is structurally supported by the AI era: more attack surface, more automation, and a secular shift toward consolidating dozens of point tools onto one platform. Recent wins include an AI-security partnership with Deutsche Telekom for Europe, underscoring the platform's international enterprise traction.Simply Wall St.· juin 2026

Capital Structure & Dilution Check

Verdict: Clean — Active Buybacks

No toxic financing. PANW carries ~811M shares outstanding and is a net buyer of its own stock: the board added $1.0B to the repurchase program in March 2026 after fully utilizing the prior $4.1B authorization, with buybacks continuing through year-end. The company has a routine S-3ASR automatic shelf on file (standard for a well-known seasoned issuer), but there is no ATM equity program, no convertible-toxic financing and no death-spiral risk. Stock-based compensation drives the GAAP operating-margin dip, but share count is flat-to-declining thanks to the buyback — dilution is not a thesis risk here.StockTitan 8-K· mars 2026
