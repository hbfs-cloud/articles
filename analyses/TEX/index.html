Business Overview

Terex Corporation is a global manufacturer of aerial work platforms (Genie brand — cherry pickers, boom lifts, scissor lifts), materials processing equipment (crushers, screens, conveyors), and mobile cranes. The company serves construction, infrastructure, utility, and mining end markets. Yahoo Finance· profile

In February 2026, Terex completed its merger with REV Group (specialty vehicles — ambulances, fire trucks, transit buses), creating a combined ~$8B revenue specialty equipment platform. The all-stock-and-cash deal increased Terex's share count by ~70% to 113.7M shares. Integration is in its early stages with $75M of run-rate synergies targeted by 2028. SEC EDGAR· 8-K Feb 2026

Terex benefits directly from the global infrastructure spending cycle, with its Genie aerial platform segment being a market leader in both rental fleet and direct sales channels. The company's materials processing division captures demand from aggregates production and recycling — both growth markets driven by urbanization and sustainability mandates.
