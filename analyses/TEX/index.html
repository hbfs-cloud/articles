Business Overview

Terex Corporation is a global manufacturer of aerial work platforms (Genie brand — cherry pickers, boom lifts, scissor lifts), materials processing equipment (crushers, screens, conveyors), and mobile cranes. The company serves construction, infrastructure, utility, and mining end markets.

Terex benefits directly from the global infrastructure spending cycle, with its Genie aerial platform segment being a market leader in both rental fleet and direct sales channels. The company's materials processing division captures demand from aggregates production and recycling — both growth markets driven by urbanization and sustainability mandates.
