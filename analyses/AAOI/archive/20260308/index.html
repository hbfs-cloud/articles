Business Overview

Applied Optoelectronics designs, manufactures, and sells fiber-optic networking products. Founded in 1997 and headquartered in Sugar Land, Texas, the company operates manufacturing facilities in the US, Taiwan, and China with 4,691 employees.

What Makes AAOI Different

Unlike fabless competitors, AAOI is vertically integrated — it designs its own laser chips (InP, GaAs) and manufactures in-house. This gives cost advantages and supply chain control for the 800G/1.6T transceiver ramp. The stock went from forgotten small-cap to AI infrastructure darling as datacenter optics demand exploded in 2025-2026.
