Business Overview

Fox Corporation is a news, sports, and entertainment company operating through three segments: Cable Network Programming (Fox News, Fox Business, FS1/FS2), Television (Fox Broadcasting, MyNetworkTV, local TV stations), and Tubi (free ad-supported streaming platform).

Fox dominates cable news ratings with Fox News consistently #1 and holds premium live sports rights including NFL, college football, MLB, and FIFA World Cup. The company's Tubi platform has emerged as a significant growth driver in the ad-supported streaming space, capitalizing on the industry shift away from subscription fatigue. Yahoo Finance· live
