D+ Neutral Moderate confidence

Rezolve is a high-growth agentic-commerce platform, but the available 2026 numbers remain preliminary and the capital history demands skepticism. Audited 2025 GAAP revenue was $46.8 million with a 66% gross margin; management then reported unaudited Q1 2026 revenue of about $60 million and preliminary H1 revenue of approximately $127 million versus $6.32 million a year earlier. It reaffirmed roughly $360 million of FY2026 revenue and a $500 million exit-ARR target, both management guidance rather than audited outcomes. The equity base changed materially: a January offering covered 62.5 million ordinary shares, while the 2025 20-F contained going-concern and material-weakness language. Management says operational equity is unnecessary, but that claim must be tested against cash flow and filed half-year accounts. The D+ grade remains appropriate. Status is wait under the September 1 event veto; the displayed $3.05 activation and August 26 pivot stop at $2.8299 remain inactive until audited or reviewed results reset the dossier.

  • ARR, MRR, contracted revenue and core-software margin are non-GAAP/company-defined and require cash-flow validation.

Business Overview

Rezolve sells software and acquired commerce capabilities that connect product discovery, conversational engagement, loyalty and checkout. Brain Commerce handles search and merchandising, brainpowa provides commerce-tuned models, and Brain Checkout links machine-led intent to merchant-approved transactions. Acquisitions materially changed the revenue base, so organic and acquired growth must be separated.

The AI transmission channel is direct: retailers pay to move customer interactions from keyword search toward conversational product discovery and transaction execution. The economic proof is audited revenue, recurring gross profit, customer retention and cash generation, not API-call volume or management-defined ARR alone.

The model breaks if preliminary revenue does not reconcile to filed accounts, acquired customers churn, integrations fail, or gross margin and cash conversion lag the reported top-line acceleration. Material weaknesses, going-concern history, warrants, ATM capacity and large share offerings raise the cost of any miss because per-share value can fall even while company revenue grows.

Crownpeak's 11,127,780 consideration shares and the large combined resale registration create holder supply but no fresh issuer cash. The approved $300M repurchase mandate remains authority, not an executed offset. Rezolve does not provide a current fully diluted point-in-time bridge that reconciles warrant exercise assumptions and Crownpeak consideration-share timing, so no synthetic total is presented.
