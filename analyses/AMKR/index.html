Business Overview

Amkor Technology is one of the largest outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) providers in the world — the company chip designers and foundries hand finished wafers to so they can be packaged, tested and shipped. Its crown jewel is advanced packaging: 2.5D/3D integration, fan-out wafer-level packaging (FOWLP) and high-density fan-out (HDFO), the exact technologies needed to stitch together the logic, memory and interconnects inside AI accelerators and HPC processors. As Moore's Law slows, packaging is where performance gains increasingly come from — and Amkor sits squarely in that pipeline. Finviz· jun 2026

The business spans communications, computing, automotive & industrial, and consumer end-markets, anchored by long-standing relationships with the leading foundries and fabless leaders. To meet AI-era demand, Amkor is building out capacity across Arizona (a flagship US advanced-packaging facility tied to the domestic chip supply-chain push), Korea and Vietnam, with 2026 capital spending guided to $2.5–3B. The stock has surged roughly +277% over the past year as the market re-rated advanced-packaging capacity as a scarce, structurally growing asset. Zacks — HDFO compute growth· 12 jun 2026

How to read this

OSAT is a thin-margin, capital-intensive business — 14% gross margins are normal for the model, so the story is operating leverage: revenue grew +27.5% while net margin held at 6.2% and EBITDA reached $1.2B. With $1.85B cash against $1.62B debt the balance sheet is net-cash. EV/EBITDA ~14 and PEG 0.76 say you are not overpaying on the operating-cash-flow base, even though the forward P/E (33.8x) and the $75.5 analyst mean target both flag that the price has run ahead of the conservative consensus.
