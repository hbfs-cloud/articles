Business Overview

Eightco Holdings (formerly Cryptyde, Inc.) was spun off from Vinco Ventures (BBIG) in June 2022. Originally focused on packaging and e-commerce logistics, the company underwent a radical transformation in late 2025, pivoting to become a technology investment vehicle focused on AI, blockchain, and digital consumer platforms.

Why the Price Is Where It Is

ORBS is priced as a speculative vehicle, not an operating business. The $179M market cap reflects a deep discount to its portfolio NAV ($50M OpenAI + $82M cash + WLD/ETH holdings). The market applies a massive conglomerate discount due to: (1) the toxic Cryptyde/BBIG legacy, (2) zero operating income from tech activities, (3) illiquidity of the OpenAI position, and (4) dependence on a single catalyst (OpenAI IPO). If the IPO materializes at the rumored $300B+ valuation, the $50M equity stake becomes transformative. If not, ORBS reverts to a cash-burning shell.
