Business Overview

Broadcom combines semiconductor solutions with infrastructure software. Q2 FY2026 semiconductor revenue was $15.01B, or 68% of sales, while infrastructure software generated $7.18B, or 32%.

The AI channel runs through custom accelerators, switching, routing, connectivity and optical components supplied into hyperscale clusters. Q2 AI semiconductor revenue of $10.8B and Q3 expectation of $16.0B show direct monetization; VMware adds software cash flow from enterprise infrastructure rather than AI hardware units.

The model breaks if a hyperscale custom program changes vendor or schedule, networking content falls, or VMware integration and customer transitions erode software economics. Acquisition debt, customer concentration and export controls amplify those risks despite enormous free cash flow. Semiconductor and infrastructure-software cash flows have different drivers: AI accelerators and networking depend on hyperscaler capex, while VMware depends on subscription conversion and retention. The thesis breaks if AI concentration rises while VMware customer attrition, integration costs or debt service consume the expected cash conversion.
