Business Overview

NVIDIA monetizes accelerated computing through GPUs and CPUs, high-speed networking, rack-scale systems, enterprise software and developer libraries. Data Center is a disclosed market platform and generated $89.0B in Q2 FY27; it sits inside Compute & Networking reporting rather than constituting a separate reportable segment.

The AI transmission channel is paid compute. More training, inference and agent workloads require accelerators, memory bandwidth, networking and CUDA-optimized software; Rubin and Spectrum-6 increase NVIDIA content per deployed AI factory rather than merely replacing one GPU generation.

The model breaks if customers digest capacity, shift meaningful workloads to internal ASICs, or cannot secure power and financing. China restrictions, foundry and packaging concentration, and a mistimed platform transition can turn exceptional demand into inventory and margin pressure.

NVIDIA's per-share test is shipment quality: operating cash must keep pace while receivables and inventory expand through Rubin. Equity-investment gains cannot substitute for accelerator, networking and systems profit.

Rubin execution must preserve gross margin while converting inventory and receivables into collected system revenue.
