Verdict Express

B+ Neutral High

Coherent Corp is the backbone of AI datacenter connectivity. The company manufactures optical transceivers, photonic components, and laser systems that move data inside and between hyperscale data centers. NVIDIA just committed $2B in investment plus multi-year purchase commitments for Coherent's next-gen optics — a de facto validation that optical interconnect is the bottleneck holding back AI scaling. With $6.3B TTM revenue growing at 17.5%, 73% earnings growth, and a Wyckoff markup phase in full swing from $88 to $299, the stock pulled back 21% from its March 4 high to $236.90. This dip offers a tactical entry into one of the strongest AI infrastructure plays on the market.

Why Buy

  • NVIDIA $2B deal — multi-year optics supply alliance locks in revenue visibility
  • +17.5% revenue growth — $6.3B TTM with 73% earnings growth trajectory
  • AI capex super-cycle — hyperscalers spending $200B+ on DC infrastructure
  • Analyst consensus $273 target (+15.3% upside) with BUY rating
  • 87.6% institutional ownership — strong smart money conviction

Why Avoid

  • $3.55B total debt — leverage from II-VI/Finisar acquisitions still heavy
  • Beta 1.91 — extremely high volatility; ATR $25/day (∼10% swings)
  • 4.66% net margins — thin profitability despite top-line strength
  • BCPE Watson (Bain Capital) — PE overhang, sold 9.8M shares recently
