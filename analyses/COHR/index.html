The company manufactures optical transceivers, photonic components, and laser systems that move data inside and between hyperscale data centers. Business Overview

Coherent in one sentence: Global leader in engineered materials, optoelectronic components, and laser systems serving the datacenter, telecom, industrial, and semiconductor markets. Formerly II-VI Incorporated, rebranded in 2022 after acquiring Finisar ($3.2B) and Coherent ($5.7B) to become a photonics powerhouse with 30,000+ employees in Saxonburg, PA.

Why Coherent matters for AI: Every NVIDIA GPU cluster needs high-bandwidth optical links to avoid becoming a data bottleneck. Coherent supplies the transceivers (800G today, 1.6T next-gen) that connect GPUs to switches and racks. NVIDIA's $2B investment signals that photonics is becoming as critical as the chips themselves. Coherent also supplies VCSELs (vertical-cavity surface-emitting lasers) used in 3D sensing and LiDAR, and SiC substrates for power electronics in EVs.
