Business Overview

TTM manufactures advanced printed circuit boards, RF components and mission-system assemblies. Revenue depends on volume, layer count, technology content and utilization across data-center networking, aerospace/defense, automotive, medical and industrial programs.

AI reaches TTM through the physical interconnect: accelerators, high-radix switches and power-dense racks require high-speed, high-layer-count boards with tighter signal-integrity tolerances. Data-center/networking mix therefore raises content per system, while aerospace/defense adds long-cycle programs and a $1.7B-plus backlog.

The model breaks if hyperscale builds pause, customers redesign boards, yields or utilization disappoint, or defense programs slip. The Epiq acquisition adds capabilities but also all-cash funding, execution and leverage risk just as the equity multiple assumes continued margin expansion. A 1.49 book-to-bill and backlog above $1.7B support visibility, yet aerospace and defense mix carries program, qualification and working-capital timing risk. The critical test is converting data-center demand into margin without allowing acquisition integration or copper and laminate costs to absorb the revenue gain.
