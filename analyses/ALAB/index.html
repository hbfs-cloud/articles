Business Overview

Astera Labs designs semiconductor connectivity for AI and cloud infrastructure. Its Intelligent Connectivity Platform spans four product families: Aries (PCIe/CXL retimers that extend high-speed signals across server racks), Taurus (smart cable modules for Ethernet scale-out), Leo (CXL memory controllers) and the newer Scorpio smart fabric switches purpose-built to connect GPU clusters. In short, when a hyperscaler builds an AI rack, Astera's silicon is the plumbing that lets thousands of accelerators talk to each other at full bandwidth — a textbook picks-and-shovels position. Astera IR· mai 2026

The business model is fabless and software-rich: Astera ships chips plus its COSMOS software suite for fleet-wide diagnostics, which deepens design-win stickiness. Content per AI server keeps climbing as scale-up (within a rack) and scale-out (across racks) fabrics grow more complex, and Scorpio expands the addressable dollar content well beyond retimers. Revenue grew +93% YoY to roughly $1.0B trailing, with a 76% gross margin — economics that look more like a software franchise than a commodity chipmaker. The flip side: that growth is fully priced in, and any deceleration in hyperscaler capex would hit the multiple hard. Macrotrends· 2026
