Verdict Express

C Neutral Moderate confidence

Strategy is no longer usefully valued as an enterprise-software vendor: the common stock is a leveraged residual claim behind 843,775 BTC, $6.67 billion of debt and $15.46 billion of preferred liquidation preference at June 30. Q2 software revenue improved 6.9% to $122.4 million, led by subscription services, but that cash engine is immaterial beside an $8.33 billion GAAP operating loss caused by bitcoin fair-value accounting. The investable question is whether repeated common and preferred issuance can add BTC per diluted share faster than dividends, interest and mNAV compression consume value. Management raised $17.06 billion through ATMs year to date and held a $3.75 billion USD reserve, providing more than 2.1 years of stated preferred-dividend and interest coverage. That reduces forced-sale risk, not structural leverage. The C grade is defensible for financing access and software recurrence, but the equity remains a complex, reflexive bitcoin vehicle. With the setup still inactive, conviction requires both the stated technical activation and evidence that incremental capital remains accretive per share.

Why Buy

  • 843,775 BTC held as of July 26, 2026; scale gives unmatched public-market bitcoin exposure.
  • Q2 software revenue rose 6.9% YoY to $122.4M; subscription services grew to $62.9M from $40.8M.
  • $3.75B USD reserve covered more than 2.1 years of stated preferred dividends and interest.
  • BTC Yield was 4.5% YTD, equivalent to management's 29,997 BTC Gain KPI.
  • $17.06B raised through ATMs YTD demonstrates exceptional capital-market access.

Why Avoid

  • Q2 GAAP operating loss was $8.33B and net loss was $8.22B, dominated by bitcoin fair-value losses.
  • $6.67B debt plus $15.46B preferred liquidation preference sits ahead of common equity.
  • Q2 preferred dividends were $400.7M; recurring fixed claims can absorb software cash generation.
  • July 26 BTC market value was $54.77B versus $63.69B aggregate cost, a $8.92B gap.
  • Common and preferred ATMs can improve treasury scale while diluting or subordinating common holders.

Business Overview

Strategy earns operating revenue from enterprise analytics licenses, subscriptions, support and consulting, but Q2 subscription services supplied $62.9M of the $122.4M software total and are the only visibly expanding recurring component. Software provides cash flow and a listed-company chassis, not the dominant equity value.

The AI transmission channel is software-side: Strategy One and Mosaic can monetize governed enterprise data and AI-assisted analytics for installed customers. It is not the equity's dominant valuation channel; bitcoin holdings, capital raised per diluted share and financing costs overwhelm software economics by orders of magnitude.

The model breaks if bitcoin falls faster than liquidity can absorb fixed claims, if mNAV closes and new securities cease to be accretive, or if preferred dividends and interest outgrow the USD reserve. Software growth cannot presently repair a treasury-scale capital loss, while accounting volatility does not eliminate that economic exposure.

Dilution Risk

Strategy's capital structure is an issuance machine, not a simple common-share count. Through July 2026 it had raised $17.06B YTD through ATM programs, while debt was $6.67B and mezzanine preferred liquidation preference was $15.46B at June 30. The $21B common ATM and separate $21B STRC preferred ATM must not be conflated: common sales dilute ownership; preferred sales add senior claims and recurring dividends. The Q2 10-Q disclosed 31.328M potential Class A shares excluded as anti-dilutive across options, awards, STRK and convertible notes. Adding those to 377.626M current basic shares gives a 408.954M minimum sensitivity, not a complete current fully diluted count because award vesting and conversion conditions can change.
