Business Overview

Fabrinet in one sentence: A precision optical, electro-mechanical, and electronic contract manufacturer headquartered in the Cayman Islands with operations in Thailand (Chonburi & Pathumthani) and Israel, serving OEMs in optical communications, industrial lasers, automotive sensors, and medical devices.

Why Fabrinet matters for AI: The AI datacenter buildout requires massive optical interconnect bandwidth — 800G and 1.6T transceivers for GPU-to-GPU communication. Fabrinet is one of the few companies with the precision manufacturing capability to produce these components at scale. Their Thailand facilities offer cost advantages over US/Japanese competitors while maintaining quality standards demanded by Cisco, Lumentum, Coherent, and other Tier-1 optical OEMs. The Aurora Innovation partnership (announced March 2026) for autonomous vehicle hardware adds a new growth vector.

Why the stock pulled back 23%

The recent correction from $633 to $489 reflects three factors: (1) broader market rotation out of high-multiple AI stocks as rates rise, (2) profit-taking after a +185% 1-year rally, and (3) the Simply Wall St article highlighting valuation concerns (DCF fair value ~$330). Despite the pullback, the fundamental thesis remains intact: 35.9% revenue growth, 4 consecutive EPS beats, and a secular AI datacenter tailwind. The question is whether the current valuation (53.6x P/E) properly discounts the growth trajectory.
