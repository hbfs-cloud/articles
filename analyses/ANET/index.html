Business Overview

Arista sells high-speed Ethernet switches, routers, campus products and EOS-based software and services to cloud, AI, data-center and enterprise customers. Product platforms drive most revenue, while software and support deepen lifetime economics.

The AI channel is network utilization: accelerator clusters need scale-up, scale-out and scale-across fabrics with high bandwidth, low latency and telemetry. Arista's 1.6 Tbps Etherlink platforms and EOS automation target the switching layer that determines whether expensive compute sits idle.

The model breaks if hyperscalers moderate capex, adopt proprietary or competing fabrics, or consolidate spending with other vendors. Customer concentration, dependence on merchant silicon and contract manufacturers, and component constraints can disrupt even a strong secular demand cycle. Software support renewals and EOS subscriptions improve visibility, but hardware still depends on cloud deployment cadence and merchant-silicon availability. The model breaks if Microsoft or Meta slows clusters, custom silicon reduces switching intensity, or campus growth cannot offset a pause in AI back-end networks.
