The Company's restricted stock units vest on 4 designated dates each quarter (each, a "Quarterly Vesting Date"). a. Your PSU award will be an award of performance restricted stock units covering shares of Company common stock having a "value" equal to $ 2,000,000 as determined in accordance with the Company's customary practices.
