Business Overview

ServiceNow sells subscriptions that digitize IT, employee, customer, security and creator workflows. Revenue grows through seats, workflow expansion, premium editions and longer enterprise commitments. Subscription revenue, cRPO and large net-new ACV transactions reveal whether the platform is broadening inside customers.

The AI transmission channel is workflow execution. AI Control Tower, agents and data context can route, govern and complete work across departments and third-party systems. Crossing $1B of AI annual contract value makes that channel measurable, while cRPO shows whether contracts persist beyond pilot projects.

The model breaks if agents remain demos, if customers resist premium pricing, or if acquisitions and hyperscaler costs erode unit economics. Q2 included federal on-premise revenue pulled forward from Q3, which flatters near-term growth. ServiceNow must convert AI contracts into durable subscription revenue while narrowing the large GAAP/non-GAAP margin gap; otherwise a premium multiple rests on adjusted economics that owners do not fully receive.
