Verdict Express

D+ Bullish Moderate confidence

Applied Digital has crossed from speculative site developer into a contracted AI datacenter landlord, but the accounting still exposes how much execution sits between signed megawatts and durable equity value. Fiscal Q4 2026 revenue rose 407% to $258.7 million, HPC revenue reached $203.0 million and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA was $42.4 million. The portfolio now claims 1,410 MW of contracted critical IT load and $36 billion of initial 15-year contracted revenue, with 175 MW live at Polaris Forge 1. Those are substantial anchors. Yet GAAP net loss attributable to common shareholders was $110.6 million, stock-based compensation in SG&A was $116.8 million, and cash plus restricted cash of $4.2 billion sits against roughly $5.0 billion of debt. Tenant fit-out contributed $152.4 million of quarterly HPC revenue, so headline growth is not equivalent to recurring base rent. APLD deserves credit for leasing traction, not a free pass on construction, customer concentration or dilution. The D+ grade remains fundamental; the stock is only a watch above $26.96.

Business Overview

Applied Digital develops and operates high-density campuses, earning recurring base rent, tenant fit-out revenue and expense recoveries from HPC customers; it also retains a smaller datacenter-hosting operation. Revenue depends on commissioned megawatts, lease commencement and construction progress.

The AI transmission channel is direct but physical: hyperscalers and model developers contract critical IT capacity, Applied Digital finances and builds the powered shell, and rent begins as halls are delivered. The $36 billion initial contracted-revenue figure is therefore a long-duration construction and occupancy schedule, not current-period sales.

The model breaks through project delays, cost overruns, unavailable power, tenant concentration or financing terms that shift too much value to creditors and preferred holders. Fit-out revenue can also flatter growth while base rent is still ramping, so segment NOI and delivered MW matter more than consolidated revenue alone. Investors should therefore reconcile recurring base rent, segment operating profit, delivered critical IT load and unrestricted parent liquidity before assigning value to the full contracted-revenue headline.

Dilution Risk

APLD is financing a multi-campus build with debt, restricted project cash and equity-linked instruments. The fiscal 2026 filing reports 318.968M non-GAAP diluted weighted-average shares, versus roughly 284.3M point-in-time basic shares. It separately discloses about 7.97M unvested RSUs and 14.39M performance units; 8,393,611 registered warrant shares and the approximately $590M preferred commitment add further legal or variable claims. The preferred conversion cannot be fixed before its contractual settlement inputs are known, so no false fully diluted total is presented. The resale S-3 covers 2.4M warrant shares held by designated purchaser affiliates and provides no issuer resale proceeds. Cash plus restricted cash of $4.2B is project-bound and cannot be freely netted against roughly $5.0B debt.
