The purchase price for the Private Placement Shares was $12.01 per share, the closing price of the Common Stock on April 30, 2026, the date immediately preceding the date of execution of the Securities Purchase Agreement, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $15.75 million, which was funded from the Reporting Person's working capital.
