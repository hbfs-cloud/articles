Analysis Summary

Applied Digital Corporation (APLD) positions itself as a key player in digital infrastructure for high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) in North America. The company recently capitalized on its strategic positioning with a $2.15 billion senior secured notes offering to fund the expansion of its Polaris Forge 2 campus, leased to Oracle. Another major development is the transaction with Ekso Bionics, which will lead to the creation of ChronoScale Corporation, a new publicly traded entity focused on cloud services.

However, APLD stock exhibits extreme volatility (Beta of 7.34), very high short interest (over 31% of float), and a financial history that, despite impressive revenue growth, is not yet generating net profits. The recent sale of NVIDIA's entire stake in APLD has also added to the pressure. The opportunity lies in its exposure to the booming AI and HPC markets and its strong partnerships. The risk is amplified by a valuation that anticipates strong growth, increasing debt, and erratic market movements.

Company Profile

Applied Digital Corporation designs, develops, and operates digital infrastructure solutions for the high-performance computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence (AI) industries in North America. The company offers infrastructure services to crypto mining customers and GPU computing solutions for critical workloads related to AI, machine learning, and other HPC tasks.

Recent News and Major Events

March 2, 2026: $2.15 Billion Senior Secured Notes Offering

Applied Digital announced, through its subsidiary APLD ComputeCo 2 LLC, its intention to offer a private placement of senior secured notes totaling $2.15 billion, due in 2031. The funds raised will finance the development and construction of 200 megawatts of critical IT capacity at its AI Factory campus Polaris Forge 2, currently leased to Oracle. This operation underscores APLD's commitment to expanding its infrastructure to meet the growing demand for AI and HPC.

February 17, 2026: Strategic Transaction with Ekso Bionics Holdings, Inc.

APLD entered into a contribution and exchange agreement with Ekso Bionics Holdings, Inc., which will see Cloud, an APLD subsidiary, become a wholly-owned subsidiary of Ekso. Ekso Bionics will change its name to ChronoScale Corporation, and the contributor will hold approximately 97% of the new combined entity. This transaction aims to create a new publicly traded company, ChronoScale Corporation, focusing on APLD's cloud activities.

March 3, 2026: NVIDIA Divests its Holdings

NVIDIA sold all its stakes in Arm Holdings and Applied Digital during the fourth fiscal quarter. For APLD, this represented 7,716,050 shares. Although NVIDIA realized substantial gains on this investment, this sale may have fueled selling pressure on the stock. NVIDIA is a major player in AI, and its investment decisions are often scrutinized.

Trade Idea: APLD

Trade Thesis: Strategic Rebound on Key Support

APLD stands at a crossroads between strong sectoral growth (AI/HPC) and a valuation that already incorporates some of this potential. Recent announcements (Oracle financing, ChronoScale) are long-term bullish catalysts. However, NVIDIA's sale and current selling pressure create a potentially attractive entry point if the key 200-day EMA support holds. This trade is a contrarian approach based on a technical rebound after a correction, with a favorable risk/reward if the thesis materializes.
