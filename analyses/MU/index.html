That operating leverage supports the 70/100 quality score, but it also makes peak-cycle normalization the central contrarian risk. Investors must distinguish those current GAAP margins from mixed-period trailing ratios and must not infer a live entry from the grade. Wait for valid market-data provenance before using the unchanged entry, stop and targets. Memory pricing, customer inventory digestion, HBM qualification and a large capital-expenditure response can reverse earnings faster than revenue headlines imply.

Business Overview

Micron manufactures DRAM, NAND and related memory products sold through Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile and Client, and Automotive and Embedded businesses. Revenue is driven by bit shipments, average selling prices, product mix and manufacturing cost per bit. Because fixed fabrication costs are high, modest changes in pricing and utilization create very large swings in gross margin and earnings.

The AI transmission channel runs through high-bandwidth memory attached to accelerators and through server DRAM and data-center SSD content. Q3 FY2026 Cloud Memory revenue of $13.769B and Core Data Center revenue of $11.524B show that AI clusters affect both premium HBM and broader memory demand. Scarcity can lift contract pricing faster than wafers can be qualified and packaged.

What breaks is the cycle. Customers can digest inventory, competitors can add capacity, yields can improve faster than demand, or accelerator architectures can alter memory content. Current 84.6% GAAP gross margin and 80.4% GAAP operating margin are quarter-specific records, not normalized economics. Capital spending, export controls and long qualification cycles can turn today’s shortage into tomorrow’s oversupply before trailing financial statements reveal the change.
