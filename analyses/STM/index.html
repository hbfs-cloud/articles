Business Overview

STMicroelectronics is one of the world's largest semiconductor companies, headquartered in Geneva with major manufacturing operations in France, Italy, and Singapore. The company produces a wide range of chips for automotive, industrial, personal electronics, and communications applications.

STM is a primary beneficiary of the EU CHIPS Act, receiving substantial subsidies for expanding fab capacity in Europe. The automotive segment (~35% of revenue) is rebounding strongly as vehicle electrification and ADAS adoption accelerate. With $12.4B in TTM revenue growing 23% YoY, STM is executing one of the strongest cyclical recoveries in the European semiconductor landscape. The company's diversified end-market exposure (auto, industrial, IoT, power) provides resilience through cycles.
