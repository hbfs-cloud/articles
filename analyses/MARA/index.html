Verdict Express

D+ Bullish Moderate confidence

MARA is an industrial bitcoin producer with treasury leverage, not an AI data-center company in current reported economics. Q2 revenue fell 27% YoY to $174.9 million despite 70.3 EH/s energized hashrate, because mining economics remained exposed to network difficulty, power cost and bitcoin price. Production improved 3% YoY to 2,422 BTC, but purchased-energy cost per coin rose 14.7% to $38,690 and GAAP net loss reached $611.3 million, including a $343 million bitcoin fair-value loss. The balance sheet carried 35,577 BTC and $421.3 million cash, yet $2.47 billion of debt and a subsequent $600 million BTC-backed facility increase common-equity convexity. Long Ridge and Matagorda create credible power-site optionality for AI/HPC, but neither is a contracted AI revenue stream in the quarter. A D+ quality grade is therefore appropriate: scale and assets are real, earnings quality and leverage are weak. The current trade remains watch-only until $11.60 confirms; bitcoin rallies cannot substitute for improving cost per hash and financing discipline.

Business Overview

MARA earns current revenue by operating bitcoin-mining fleets: realized BTC price, coins produced, network difficulty, fleet efficiency and power cost determine gross economics. Q2 production was 2,422 BTC from 70.3 EH/s energized hashrate, but revenue still fell because capacity did not offset weaker price-volume economics. It sold 2,213 BTC at an average $73,078, making treasury-sale policy another cash-flow variable.

The AI transmission channel is physical power infrastructure. Long Ridge and prospective Matagorda capacity could host HPC loads, but conversion requires interconnection, cooling, customer contracts, construction capital and service-level reliability; no Q2 AI revenue was reported and no site right should be modeled as rent.

The model breaks when hashprice falls below fleet-and-power cost, collateralized BTC declines, curtailment or interconnection delays reduce utilization, or debt-funded expansion fails to produce per-share cash flow. Treasury appreciation can mask weak mining unit economics temporarily, while pledged coins become less available precisely when financing tightens.
