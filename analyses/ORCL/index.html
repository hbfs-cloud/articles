Oracle's AI demand is real; its funding problem is equally real. Q4 FY2026 revenue rose 21% to $19.2B, OCI climbed 93% to $5.8B and RPO reached $638B. Backlog becomes equity value only after Oracle finances and powers data centers, then collects cloud revenue. FY2026 operating cash flow was $32B, yet free cash flow was negative $23.7B. Business Overview

Oracle earns recurring support, cloud applications, database subscriptions and infrastructure consumption. The installed database estate anchors renewals and migration, while OCI supplies the fastest growth. RPO captures signed cloud commitments, but recognition depends on delivering capacity over contract terms.

AI demand reaches Oracle through GPU clusters, networking, database access and sovereign cloud regions. OCI revenue rises after campuses obtain power, hardware is installed and customers accept service. Oracle can cross-sell database and applications if utilization earns an adequate return on construction.

The break point is capital intensity. Oracle spent enough in FY2026 to drive free cash flow to negative $23.7B and plans debt plus equity financing for the next build phase. Delayed power, concentrated counterparties, weak utilization or higher funding costs can turn $638B RPO into leverage and dilution before profitable revenue.

Database support cash flow can subsidize part of the build, but it cannot neutralize unlimited construction spending. Investors need campus-level delivery, utilization and financing disclosures to judge whether OCI growth is compounding franchise value or merely enlarging the balance sheet.

Dilution Risk

Oracle had roughly 2.88B common shares outstanding. The February 2 ATM authorized up to $20B of primary common sales, but the May 31 Form 10-K states no shares had been sold, leaving full capacity. Oracle separately issued 100M depositary shares for $5B gross, representing 50,000 Series D mandatory-convertible preferred shares. Conversion on January 15, 2029 implies 24.99M-31.24M common shares, producing a minimum potential denominator of approximately 2.905B-2.911B before stock-paid dividends and employee awards. The exact theoretical fully diluted count remains unavailable; ATM capacity is authorized but not issued equity.
