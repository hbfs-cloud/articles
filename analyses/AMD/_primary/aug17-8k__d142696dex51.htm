To the addressee set forth above:

We have acted as special counsel to Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), in connection with the issuance of $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.600% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Notes”), $1,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.000% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Notes”), $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.250% Senior Notes due 2033 (the “2033 Notes”) and $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.500% Senior Notes due 2036 (together with the 2029 Notes, the 2031 Notes and the 2033 Notes, the “Notes”) under that certain Indenture, dated June 9, 2022 (the “Base Indenture”), between the Company and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as trustee (the “Trustee”), as supplemented by that certain Third Supplemental Indenture, dated August 17, 2026, between such parties (the “Supplemental Indenture” and, together with the Base Indenture, the “Indenture”) and pursuant to a registration statement on Form S-3 under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Act”), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “Commission”) on August 13, 2026 (Registration No. 333-298288) (as so filed, the “Registration Statement”), a base prospectus, dated August 13, 2026, included in the Registration Statement at the time it originally became effective (the “Base Prospectus”), a preliminary prospectus supplement, dated August 13, 2026, filed with the Commission pursuant to Rule 424(b) under the Act (together with the Base Prospectus, the “Preliminary Prospectus”), the document that the Issuer has identified as an “issuer free writing prospectus” (as defined in Rule 433 and Rule 405 under the Act) (the “Specified IFWP”), a final prospectus supplement, dated August 13, 2026, filed with the Commission pursuant to Rule 424(b) under the Act on August 14, 2026 (together with the Base Prospectus, the “Prospectus”), and an underwriting agreement, dated August 13, 2026, between the underwriters named therein and the Company (the “Underwriting Agreement”).
