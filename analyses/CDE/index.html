Verdict Express

B Neutral Moderate confidence

Coeur is now a scaled North American precious-metals producer whose operating improvement is visible in cash, not merely commodity marks. Q2 revenue rose 126% YoY to $1.09 billion, adjusted EBITDA reached $478.3 million at a 44% margin and free cash flow was $387.5 million. Gold production increased 51% to 163,490 ounces, while silver production declined 7% to 4.4 million ounces, making the enlarged company more gold-sensitive than its legacy identity suggests. The balance sheet moved to $347 million net cash, and management's full-year framework calls for roughly 690,000 gold ounces, 20 million silver ounces, $2.3 billion adjusted EBITDA and $1.5 billion free cash flow under its stated metal-price assumptions. The contrarian issue is integration: shares rose from 642.1 million at year-end to 1.03 billion after acquisitions, and Rainy River plus New Afton guidance deteriorated. A B grade is defensible because current cash generation offsets dilution and execution risk. The trade stays in Wait until a completed 15-minute close above $22.45 prints with non-zero regular-session volume; elevated metal prices are already embedded in margins.

Business Overview

Coeur earns revenue by mining and selling gold, silver and copper from a larger post-acquisition portfolio. Q2 gold production was 163,490 ounces, silver production 4.4M ounces and copper production 11.4M pounds; realized prices, grades, recoveries and unit costs set mine margins. The quarter generated $387.5M of free cash flow and left the company with $347M net cash.

The AI transmission channel is through metals and infrastructure, not software. Data-center construction and electrification can support copper and silver demand, but gold still supplied roughly two-thirds of Q2 metal revenue and monetary demand remains the dominant valuation driver.

The model breaks if grades, recoveries or ramp-ups miss plan, if Rainy River or New Afton costs remain elevated, or if gold and silver prices normalize before acquisition synergies arrive. Large purchase-accounting charges and the enlarged share count can also obscure whether growth is accretive per share.

Risk Analysis

Risk Profile: Moderate

The main risk is that strong metal prices conceal mine-level execution weakness: acquisition dilution, higher Rainy River and New Afton costs, reserve depletion and commodity normalization could reduce free cash flow per share despite higher consolidated production. Retail war-room: gold/silver moves or mine guidance can gap CDE through $20.36; use regular-session liquidity and limit orders to control spread and slippage, scale sizing for commodity discontinuity, avoid earnings and reserve-update events, and do not chase above the $22.45 activation or after a metal-price spike.
