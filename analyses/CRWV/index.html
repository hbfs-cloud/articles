CoreWeave is the leading pure-play AI cloud infrastructure company in the US. Originally founded as Atlantic Crypto Corporation in 2017, it pivoted to GPU cloud computing in 2019 and now operates NVIDIA-powered data centers at scale. Revenue surged 110% YoY to $5.13B in FY2025, with 71.7% gross margins. However, the stock has plunged 60% from its June 2025 ATH of ~$187, hammered by concerns over $14.9B in capex, $29.8B in debt, and a Q4 earnings miss on Feb 26. At $72.90, the stock trades at 12.7x EV/Revenue — a steep discount to its growth profile. NVIDIA holds a 10% stake. Analyst consensus target is $121.

The debt question is THE question for CRWV: With $29.8B in debt against $5.1B in revenue, CoreWeave is one of the most leveraged companies in the tech sector. This debt is primarily used to finance GPU purchases and data center construction. The bet is that AI compute demand will continue to grow exponentially, allowing CoreWeave to service this debt through rapidly growing revenue. If AI demand stalls or CoreWeave loses competitive positioning, the debt burden becomes existential. The IPO raised ~$1.5B, but the company needs continued access to capital markets. Dilution risk is moderate — the company has preferred debt financing over equity dilution so far, but this could change if debt markets tighten.
