Business Overview

Zscaler sells cloud-delivered Zero Trust subscriptions that connect users, devices and workloads to applications without exposing the underlying network. Revenue expands through customer additions, seats, data protection, workload security and higher platform bundles. ARR and net-new ARR are the most direct demand measures.

The AI transmission channel is access control for users, agents, data and applications. AI agents create non-human identities and more east-west communication that must be inspected and governed. Zscaler can monetize this through Zero Trust Exchange, data security, Red Canary and access-graph capabilities rather than selling generic AI infrastructure.

The model breaks if customers standardize on bundled Microsoft or network-vendor security, if acquired products fail to cross-sell, or if capex rises faster than consumption. Red Canary already lifts headline ARR above the organic rate. Zscaler must preserve renewal, net-new ARR and cash margin while moving into broader security operations; otherwise platform expansion produces accounting complexity without proportional per-share value.
