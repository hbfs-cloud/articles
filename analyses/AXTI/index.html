Business Overview

AXT in one sentence: AXT designs, manufactures, and sells compound semiconductor substrates (Indium Phosphide, Gallium Arsenide, Germanium) — the specialized wafers on which chips for fiber optics, 5G, lasers, LEDs, and solar cells are fabricated. Headquartered in Fremont, CA, with manufacturing in China. AXT.com

Why InP is the crown jewel: As AI workloads explode, data centers need faster optical interconnects. The transition from 400G to 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers requires InP-based lasers and photodetectors. AXT is one of only three companies globally (alongside Sumitomo Electric and JX Nippon) capable of producing InP substrates at scale. This near-oligopoly positioning is what drove the parabolic rally — the market is pricing in a multi-year InP demand supercycle.

AXTI in the optical supply chain: AXT sits at the very bottom of the semiconductor optical supply chain. It produces the wafers, which companies like AAOI, LITE, COHR, and II-VI buy to fabricate laser chips and optical transceivers. These transceivers then go to hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta) for their AI data center networks. This upstream positioning means AXT benefits from ALL optical transceiver demand, regardless of which transceiver maker wins. The stock's highest correlation is with IWM (0.39), reflecting its small-cap nature.
