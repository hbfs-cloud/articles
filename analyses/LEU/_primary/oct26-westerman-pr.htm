Centrus Acquires Westerman Holdings, LLC, Bringing a Key Supplier In-House and Diversifying Its Manufacturing Capacity October 19, 2026 Secures a key centrifuge component supplier and best-in-class manufacturer with an existing and growing business Diversifies revenue base by expanding Centrus' addressable market with significant adjacent, non-nuclear manufacturing work Drives continued growth for both organizations through commercial and operational synergies and cost savings BETHESDA, Md., Oct. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Centrus Energy Corp. (NYSE: LEU) ("Centrus"), a trusted supplier of nuclear fuel and services, today announced that it has acquired Westerman Holdings, LLC ("Westerman"), one of Centrus' largest and most diversified suppliers, for approximately $116 million, funded through a combination of approximately $95 million cash on hand and $21 million of equity. The transaction is expected to be accretive to adjusted earnings per share in the first year following closing. The acquisition represents a significant milestone for Centrus as it builds supply chain resilience while diversifying its revenue base. Westerman's expertise strengthens and de-risks Centrus' supply chain by securing and bringing in-house its critical manufacturing capabilities. The acquisition simultaneously broadens Centrus' manufacturing platform, customer base, and future revenue opportunities through commercial and operational synergies. The addition of Westerman supports Centrus' previously announced multi-billion-dollar expansion of uranium enrichment capacity. Westerman is an existing manufacturer for centrifuge casings and other major centrifuge components for Centrus. With the acquisition, Centrus intends to secure this key supplier while expanding Westerman's capabilities. Westerman currently services a number of non-nuclear customers across a variety of industries including oil and gas as well as other high-growth industrial sectors, diversifying Centrus' revenue base beyond its core nuclear business. The acquisition is expected to result in lower project costs for Centrus and greater efficiencies with an integrated supply chain. "Westerman is a highly regarded, best-in-class supplier and fabricator for Centrus, the broader nuclear industry, and a number of other sectors," said Amir Vexler, President and CEO of Centrus. "Bringing Westerman in-house demonstrates our commitment to securing our supply chain to support our build-out while adding to our capabilities and expanding our customer base.  We are committed to growing and expanding Westerman alongside our enrichment business, and are confident that this transaction will unlock significant value for our shareholders."  "Westerman has spent more than a century building advanced, differentiated manufacturing capabilities," said Jacob Garrett, CEO of Westerman, who will now transition to President of Westerman and also become a Vice President of Centrus Energy. "Joining with Centrus will allow us to accelerate that trajectory. Together, we can expand our production, deepen our nuclear manufacturing portfolio, and create new opportunities for our employees, our customers, and our communities. We are excited for the future we

 
will build together." Centrus acquired all of Westerman's business lines and Westerman has become a subsidiary within Centrus. Westerman will continue to operate under the Westerman name, leveraging its brand identity and customer relationships. Founded in 1909, Westerman is an established, high-quality industrial fabrication manufacturer with specialized capabilities serving the nuclear, oil and gas, and broader high-growth industrial markets. With approximately 400 employees and four world-class facilities in Ohio, Oklahoma, and Wyoming, Westerman provides a broad portfolio of products that span upstream and midstream oil and gas production equipment, engineered industrial systems, and nuclear‑grade products including UF6 cylinders. Barclays served as financial advisor to Centrus and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz served as legal counsel to Centrus for this transaction. About Centrus Centrus Energy is a trusted American supplier of nuclear fuel and services for the nuclear power industry, helping meet the growing need for clean, affordable, carbon-free energy. Since 1998, the Company has provided its utility customers with more than 1,850 reactor years of fuel, which is equivalent to more than 7 billion tons of coal. With world-class technical and engineering capabilities, Centrus is pioneering production of High-Assay, Low-Enriched Uranium and is leading the effort to restore America's uranium enrichment capabilities at scale so that we can meet our clean energy, energy security, and national security needs.
