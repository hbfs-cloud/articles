Verdict Express

D Bearish Held at D — refreshed on 4 Aug closes

Six weeks on, the specific thing we warned about has not happened, and that is the most useful fact in this update. Share count is unchanged at 49,968,344. The White Lion facility has not been drawn in any way that shows up in the share register. Short interest has halved, from 10.84% of float to 6.76%. The bears left and the dilution did not arrive. The stock still fell 12.9%, to $0.4942. What is actually eroding this equity is silence. EON has not filed anything with the SEC since 28 April. The FY2025 annual report was due 15 April and is 112 days late. NYSE American set the cure deadline at 15 October, 71 days out. Meanwhile the company has issued three operational press releases since late June, including a 2026-2030 capital plan, none of which it can support with audited accounts.

Why Buy

  • Book value $1.352 a share against a $0.4942 quote, a 63% discount, wider than the 54% we noted in June. Whether that book is real is the whole question, and the audit that would answer it is 112 days late.
  • The dilution thesis has not played out. Shares outstanding are flat at 49,968,344 since our June note. Whatever the $150M White Lion facility permits on paper, it has not been used to print stock this quarter.
  • Short interest halved to 6.76% of float, 3.18M shares. The crowded bearish position we flagged in June has largely closed. Less forced selling, but also less squeeze fuel.
  • Operationally the company keeps reporting activity: a South Justis petrophysics study on 27 July, a 2026-2030 growth and capex plan on 30 June, a drilling programme update on 26 June.

Why Avoid

  • No SEC filing of any kind since 28 April 2026, 99 days of silence. The last periodic report covers the quarter ended 30 September 2025.
  • FY2025 Form 10-K was due 15 April 2026 and has not been filed. NYSE Regulation issued the noncompliance notice on 16 April. The initial cure period ends 15 October 2026.
  • Because the annual report is outstanding, the Q1 and Q2 2026 quarterly reports are missing too. No audited or reviewed statement covers any period after September 2025.
  • The February 2026 Item 4.02 filing declared prior financial statements unreliable. That restatement is unresolved and is the stated reason the audit has not closed.
  • The company is publishing a five-year capital plan while unable to file last year accounts. Treat every operational figure in those releases as unaudited management assertion.

Thesis

Nothing here is investable, but the reason has moved. In June the case against EONR was imminent dilution through a death-spiral facility. That mechanism has not fired. The share count has not moved in six weeks and the shorts have gone home. What replaced it is duller and harder to escape: the company cannot close its audit. No annual report for 2025, no quarterly report for either 2026 quarter, no filing of any kind since 28 April, and a listing deadline on 15 October. You cannot underwrite a balance sheet nobody has signed. Anyone still holding from the April entry at $0.83 is down 40.4%.

Catalysts

  • FY2025 Form 10-K filed before 15 October. This resolves the listing deficiency and makes the balance sheet knowable, and it is the single event that would let anyone re-underwrite the name.
  • Cure period lapses on 15 October without a filing. NYSE American may then grant a further six months at its discretion, or begin delisting proceedings.
  • Any 424B3 prospectus supplement, or a share count above 49,968,344, would show the White Lion facility being drawn after all.
  • A going-concern qualification when the audit does land.

Invalidation

  • Delisting to OTC Pink Sheets — remaining thesis dies
  • Additional 424B3 prospectus supplement filing — confirms ELOC drawdown, more dilution incoming
  • Going-concern opinion from new auditor in next 10-K
  • Cash balance drops below $500K without announced financing
