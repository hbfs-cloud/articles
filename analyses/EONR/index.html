LULD Halts — How They Work on OTC Stocks

LULD (Limit Up / Limit Down) is an SEC mechanism that pauses trading when a stock moves more than a set percentage within a 5-minute rolling window. For OTC stocks priced under $1, the threshold is 20% in 5 minutes. EONR was already down ~16% from yesterday's close in early trading. The 12:30 press release created buying pressure, but if the stock moved too fast in either direction, LULD would pause trading for 5 minutes minimum. On OTC, halts can extend longer. The telltale sign: massive volume candle immediately at resume — trapped sellers and new buyers converge at once. Today's 1.94M share candle at 13:30 is a textbook halt-resume signature.

Is This Manipulation?

Unclear. Two scenarios: (1) Legitimate LULD halts — normal circuit breakers triggered by the stock's volatility; perfectly legal and protective. (2) Coordinated press release timing — the 12:30 PR was published exactly when the stock was at its daily low ($0.729), which is suspicious timing. If insiders knew the press release was coming, buying before the announcement would be illegal. We cannot confirm either scenario, but the pattern warrants caution. Always verify halt status on OTC Markets or FINRA halt lists.
