Verdict Express

D Bearish Held at D — rebuilt on 4 August closes

Our June note put a D on EON and said the danger was dilution through a $150m equity facility. Six weeks later the stock is at $0.4942, down 12.5% from the 9 June close of $0.5649 and 40.4% from the April entry at $0.83. The dilution never came. Shares outstanding are 49,968,344, the same figure as in June. Short interest fell from 10.84% of float to 7.6%. And company insiders were buyers, not sellers: 28 open-market purchases against 2 sales, including the chief executive, the chief financial officer and two directors. We were right on direction and wrong on mechanism. The real problem is that nobody can check the accounts. EON has filed nothing with the SEC since 28 April. The FY2025 annual report was due 15 April and is 112 days late. No balance sheet has been filed for any date after 30 September 2025. NYSE Regulation has set the cure deadline at 15 October, 71 days out.

Why Buy

  • The dilution thesis did not play out. Shares outstanding are 49,968,344, unchanged since June. Whatever the equity facility permits on paper, it has not printed stock this quarter.
  • Insiders bought. 28 open-market purchases against 2 sales over the trailing year: director Joseph Salvucci Sr took 118,237 shares at $0.43 on 26 November 2025 and 100,000 at $0.38 in September; CFO Mitchell Trotter 120,000 at $0.36 and 25,000 at $0.43; CEO Dante Caravaggio 10,000 at $0.36; VP Finance Mark Williams across three separate purchases. All below today's price. The buying stopped in November, before the restatement.
  • Short interest fell from 10.84% of float to 7.6%, or 3,182,475 shares. The crowded bearish position we flagged in June has largely closed.
  • Stated book value is $1.352 a share against a $0.4942 quote, a 63% discount. That is only an argument if the book is real, which is precisely what cannot be checked.
  • The company keeps reporting operational activity: a South Justis petrophysics study on 27 July, a 2026-2030 growth and capital plan on 30 June, a drilling programme update on 26 June.

Why Avoid

  • No SEC filing of any kind since 28 April 2026. The most recent periodic report covers the quarter ended 30 September 2025.
  • The FY2025 Form 10-K was due 15 April 2026 and has not been filed. NYSE Regulation issued the noncompliance notice on 16 April; the initial cure period ends 15 October 2026.
  • Because the annual report is outstanding, the Q1 and Q2 2026 quarterly reports are missing too. There is no audited or reviewed statement covering any period after September 2025.
  • A February 2026 filing declared the company's own prior financial statements unreliable. That restatement is unresolved and is the stated reason the audit has not closed.
  • We cannot tell you what this company owes. Our own June note published total debt of $44.1m citing a Form 10-K/A. No 10-K/A exists among this issuer's filings. The market data feed carries $5.39m and reconciles internally to the dollar. Both cannot be right, and no filed balance sheet since September 2025 can settle it.
  • A five-year capital plan from an issuer that cannot produce last year's audited accounts is a press release, not a plan. Every operational figure in those announcements is management describing itself with nobody checking.

Business Overview

EON Resources is a small oil and gas producer operating the Grayburg-Jackson field in the Permian Basin of south-eastern New Mexico, acquired through a 2023 SPAC combination. The asset is a mature conventional waterflood in the San Andres formation rather than a shale play, so the economics turn on workover and recompletion of existing wellbores more than on new horizontal drilling.

In September 2025 the company announced a farmout with Virtus Oil & Gas under which Virtus funds initial horizontal wells with EON carried, and management has since described a multi-well programme running to 2030. None of that activity has been reported in an audited financial statement. The last filed accounts cover the quarter ended 30 September 2025 and the FY2025 annual report has not been filed, so every operating figure published since is management assertion.

Thesis

There is no position to take here, and the reason has changed since June. Then, the case against EON was imminent dilution through an equity facility. That did not happen: the share count has not moved in six weeks, the shorts have covered, and insiders were buying below today's price as recently as November. What replaced it is duller and harder to escape. The company cannot close its audit. No annual report for 2025, no quarterly report for either 2026 quarter, nothing filed since 28 April, and a listing deadline on 15 October. We cannot even tell you what it owes: our own June debt figure cited a filing that does not exist, and the feed's figure is eight times smaller. A 63% discount to book means nothing when the book is the unaudited number. Anyone still holding from the April entry at $0.83 is down 40.4%.

Catalysts

  • FY2025 Form 10-K filed before 15 October. It resolves the listing deficiency, settles the debt question and makes the balance sheet analysable. It is the one event that would let anyone re-underwrite this.
  • Cure period lapses on 15 October without a filing. NYSE American may then grant a further six months at its discretion, or begin delisting proceedings.
  • A share count above 49,968,344, or a new prospectus supplement, would show the equity facility being drawn after all.
  • A going-concern qualification when the audit lands.
  • Renewed insider buying. The last purchase on file is 26 November 2025, three months before the restatement was declared.

Invalidation

  • The 10-K lands clean and confirms the lower debt figure, in which case the discount to book becomes analysable and this note is too bearish
  • A share count above 49,968,344, in which case the June dilution thesis was early rather than wrong
  • A close below $0.267, the 52-week low, in which case the market has stopped waiting for the filing
