Verdict Express

D+ Neutral Moderate confidence

IREN's AI cloud transition is commercially meaningful, but the August 27 results reset the operating baseline and leave the chart without a post-event base. FY2026 revenue rose to $707.0 million from $501.0 million, with AI cloud revenue surging to $128.8 million from $16.4 million. Q4 AI cloud revenue more than doubled sequentially to $70.5 million while mining revenue fell to $66.7 million. The cost is visible: FY GAAP net loss was $702.6 million after $638.8 million of impairment, Q4 non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA was only $19.2 million, and the filing reports $5.896 billion cash, $1.724 billion restricted cash and roughly $7.593 billion debt. Management's $4 billion contracted ARR is non-GAAP and may materially exceed recognized revenue; $2.8 billion GPU financing covers 90% of associated capex but adds obligations. About $1 billion had been sold under a $6 billion ATM. The D+ grade remains a quality judgment; trade status is wait until regular-session price discovery forms a new base.

Business Overview

IREN operates bitcoin mining sites and is redeploying power, datacenter shells and capital toward GPU cloud services. Revenue is driven by hash production and bitcoin economics in mining, and by commissioned accelerators, utilization and contract delivery in AI cloud.

The AI transmission channel is direct: secured power supports GPU clusters, customer contracts and financing fund accelerator purchases, and cloud revenue begins as installed systems enter service. Q4 AI cloud revenue of $70.5M versus $66.7M of mining revenue shows the transition reaching operating scale.

The model breaks if contracted ARR fails to convert into recognized revenue, GPU economics compress, financing obligations outrun deployment, or mining cash flow deteriorates during the transition. The $638.8M annual impairment proves specialized hardware can lose value quickly. Investors must distinguish $5.896B of cash from $1.724B of restricted cash and compare both with roughly $7.593B of debt before assigning value to future cloud capacity.
