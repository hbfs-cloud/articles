Verdict Express

C- Neutral Moderate confidence

Coinbase remains the highest-quality operating business in this crypto group, but Q2 exposed why a C- grade can coexist with strategic relevance. Total revenue fell 19% YoY to $1.22 billion as transaction revenue declined 22%, while subscription and services supplied 48% of net revenue and softened the volume shock. The platform still holds $246 billion of customer assets, roughly 11.2% of total crypto market capitalization, and average USDC balances on Coinbase products reached $20 billion. Yet GAAP net loss was $359.5 million, stock compensation was $238.3 million and rebuilt current EV is about 25.0x the issuer's roughly $1.878 billion trailing adjusted EBITDA on both Class A and Class B shares. Q2 adjusted EBITDA remained positive at $207.8 million, but Q3 subscription-and-services guidance of $500-$580 million does not remove sensitivity to rates, stablecoin economics or spot volumes. Buybacks have offset more than 85% of stock-compensation issuance since Q4 2024, which is useful but not free. Publication stance: structurally advantaged exchange and custody infrastructure, financially cyclical equity, and no chase before the existing activation confirms.

Why Buy

  • $246B Assets on Platform equaled 11.2% of total crypto market capitalization.
  • Subscription and services generated $555.1M, 48% of Q2 net revenue.
  • Average USDC held in Coinbase products reached a record $20B.
  • Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA was positive at $207.8M for a 14th consecutive quarter.
  • $8.61B cash and equivalents plus remaining $2B repurchase authorization provide flexibility.

Why Avoid

  • Q2 total revenue fell 19% YoY and transaction revenue fell 22% YoY.
  • GAAP net loss was $359.5M, or -$1.36 per diluted share.
  • $238.3M Q2 stock compensation remains a material recurring owner cost.
  • About 25.0x rebuilt current EV/trailing adjusted EBITDA still leaves limited margin for another volume or rate miss.
  • Stablecoin revenue of $292M depends on USDC balances, rates and commercial sharing economics.

Business Overview

Coinbase earns transaction fees from consumer and institutional trading plus subscription and services revenue from stablecoins, blockchain rewards, custody, interest and other products. Q2 transaction revenue was $599.2M and subscription and services was $555.1M, giving the company two distinct but still crypto-sensitive engines.

The AI transmission channel is indirect but real: AI agents can use Coinbase's payments, wallet and developer infrastructure for machine-to-machine settlement, while prediction markets and tokenized assets expand transaction surfaces. No current earnings line isolates AI revenue, so this remains adoption optionality rather than a forecast or present valuation pillar.

The model breaks when crypto volume and volatility contract together, stablecoin balances or rates fall, regulation raises compliance cost, or fee compression outruns product diversification. Cybersecurity, custody or compliance failure would attack the trust asset underpinning all segments and could remove customers faster than expense reductions protect margins.
