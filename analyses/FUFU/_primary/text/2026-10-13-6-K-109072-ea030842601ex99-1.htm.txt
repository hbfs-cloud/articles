Update on Proposed Acquisition of Bitcoin Mining Facility in Ethiopia: As previously disclosed on October 22, 2024, the Company, through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Finfront Holding Company, entered into an acquisition agreement (as amended and supplemented from time to time, the “Agreement”) with certain shareholders (the “Sellers”) of an Ethiopian company which owns and operates an 80-megawatt (MW) Bitcoin mining facility in Ethiopia (the “Target Company”), pursuant to which the Company agreed to acquire a majority stake in the Target Company (the “Proposed Acquisition”). The closing of the Proposed Acquisition is subject to a number of conditions precedent designated to protect the Company’s interests, including but not limited to the Target Company’s completion of certain asset consolidation, the Sellers’ completion of the required government filings in Ethiopia within the applicable timeframes, and the absence of any material adverse change in or undisclosed liability of the Target Company (the “Conditions Precedent”). As of the date hereof, the Conditions Precedent have not all been satisfied or waived by the Company, and the Proposed Acquisition has not been consummated. In connection with the Proposed Acquisition, the Company has made cash payments in the aggregate amount of US$4,100,000 to the Sellers and has deployed approximately 13,700 units of BITMAIN ANTMINER S19 series mining machines (the “Deployed Machines”) to the mining facility related to the Target Company. The Deployed Machines collectively generate an aggregate hashrate of approximately 1.57 EH/s and carry a total net book value of US$1.74 million as of September 30, 2026, representing a small proportion of the Company’s total assets. The Company has recently become aware that the Target Company is involved in a contractual dispute with its electric power supplier in Ethiopia (the “Supplier”). The Company is not a direct party to the underlying contract or dispute. On September 28, 2026, the Supplier notified the Target Company that, if the Target Company did not make certain payment to the Supplier within a ten (10)-day notice period, the Supplier intended to exercise certain security rights and take possession of the Target Company’s collateralized equipment and assets upon the expiration of such ten (10)-day notice period. To the best of the Company’s knowledge, as of the date hereof, the Target Company has not remitted the disputed amounts to the Supplier. Considering this, the Company promptly initiated an assessment of its response. Based on this assessment, the Company believes there is a material risk that the Target Company may be unable to satisfy the relevant Conditions Precedent. Such failure could prevent the closing of the Proposed Acquisition on the currently contemplated terms, within the anticipated timeframe, or at all. In addition, if the dispute results in a change to the operating environment of the Deployed Machines, part of the Company’s mining operation arrangements may need to be adjusted accordingly; however, the Company believes it has the ability to address such changes through the reallocation of its existing resources. To safeguard the security of its existing investments, the Company is currently evaluating its contractual rights and available remedies under the Agreement, including potential termination of the Agreement, commencement of arbitration against the Sellers, recovery of amounts paid and return of the Deployed Machines. The Company has full confidence in its ability to protect its legal and financial interests, as well as those of its shareholders.
