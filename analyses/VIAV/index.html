Viavi Solutions is a network infrastructure play riding the 5G, AI, and fiber optic buildout wave. The company provides test & measurement equipment critical for deploying next-generation networks, plus optical security products (anti-counterfeiting, 3D sensing). After a massive +150% rally from August 2025 lows ($11), VIAV trades at $27.60 — near its analyst consensus target of $27.21. Four consecutive earnings beats signal genuine fundamental improvement, but the stock is now priced for perfection with an EV/EBITDA of 35.9x.
