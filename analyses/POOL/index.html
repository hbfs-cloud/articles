Business Overview

Pool Corporation is the world's largest wholesale distributor of swimming pool supplies, equipment, and related leisure products. With over 420 sales centers across North America, Europe, and Australia, the company serves pool builders, retail stores, and service companies through a distribution network that handles everything from chemicals and equipment to outdoor living products.

Revenue of $5.4B is driven by a combination of maintenance/repair (non-discretionary, recurring) and new construction (discretionary, cyclical). The maintenance segment provides revenue stability — pools need chemicals and equipment regardless of economic conditions — while new construction adds cyclical upside. POOL benefits from an installed base of 5.7M+ in-ground pools in the US, each requiring ~$1,200/year in maintenance spending. The stock's decline from $345 to $192 reflects housing market headwinds on the new construction side.
