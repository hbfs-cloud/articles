ETF Overview

XLV is the SPDR Health Care Select Sector ETF, tracking the S&P 500 Healthcare sector. With $41B in AUM and a 0.09% expense ratio, it provides diversified exposure to the largest US healthcare companies across pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, medical devices, healthcare services, and life sciences.

The fund is heavily weighted toward mega-cap pharma (LLY, JNJ, ABBV, MRK) and managed care (UNH), making it a quality-defensive play. In EARLY RISK-OFF environments, XLV historically outperforms the broader market as investors seek sectors with inelastic demand and pricing power. The GLP-1 drug revolution (led by Eli Lilly at 11.2% weight) adds a secular growth dimension to the traditionally defensive thesis.
