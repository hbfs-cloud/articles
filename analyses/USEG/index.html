🏭 Business Model — The Pivot

U.S. Energy Corp (founded 1980, NASDAQ since then) was historically an oil & gas E&P company. Over 2023-2025 they made a dramatic strategic pivot: sold off oil assets, acquired the Kevin Dome Industrial Gas Field in Montana, and are now positioning as an industrial gas + carbon management company. Yahoo Finance

📍 The Kevin Dome Project — Core Asset

⛰️
Kevin Dome — Montana Industrial Gas Field
Development Stage — Pre-Revenue

Located in Montana, Kevin Dome contains a unique combination of: natural helium (rare globally, very high value), CO₂ (for carbon capture/sequestration), and natural gas. The project is developing both an industrial gas production component and a Big Sky Carbon Hub — a CCUS facility where companies can pay to sequester their CO₂ emissions underground. GlobeNewsWire · Feb 2026

🌿
Big Sky Carbon Hub — CCUS Facility
FID Taken Mar 18, 2026 ✅

Final Investment Decision taken March 18, 2026 — company committed to building the facility, targeting commercial operations Q1 2027. Revenue model: industrial emitters pay USEG to permanently store their CO₂ underground. The IRS 45Q tax credit ($85/ton CO₂ sequestered) is a key economic driver. GlobeNewsWire · Mar 18

💡 The FCF Problem

In FY2024, FCF was only -$0.75M — nearly breakeven. FY2025 collapsed to -$17M because they started investing heavily in Kevin Dome development. That's actually by design — but it means they burned through all their cash ($7.72M → $0.43M) and needed the March 2026 equity raise just to continue operations. The $7-8M raised from the 8.8M share offering barely covers ~5-6 months of current burn rate. More dilution is likely in 2026 before the Carbon Hub generates revenue in 2027.
