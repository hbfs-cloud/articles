Business Overview

Arcosa is an infrastructure products company serving the construction, engineered structures, and transportation markets. The company operates through three segments: Construction Products (aggregates, specialty materials, recycled aggregates), Engineered Structures (utility and wind towers, storage tanks), and Transportation Products (inland barges, steel components).

Arcosa holds near-monopoly positions in key regional aggregates markets, providing significant pricing power. The company is a direct beneficiary of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (IIJA), with federal infrastructure spending entering its peak disbursement phase in 2026.
