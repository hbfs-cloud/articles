Business Overview

Hewlett Packard Enterprise is a global provider of enterprise IT infrastructure spanning servers, storage, networking, and hybrid cloud. Its flagship segments — Server (including AI systems and HPE GreenLake as-a-service), Hybrid Cloud, and Intelligent Edge — sell the compute, storage, and connectivity that power corporate data centers. The 2026 close of the Juniper Networks acquisition fundamentally re-weighted the company toward high-margin Networking, directly challenging Cisco in enterprise switching and AI fabric Seeking Alpha· 2026.

The investment thesis is straightforward: HPE is a scaled, profitable beneficiary of the AI buildout that the market still prices like legacy hardware. AI systems orders hit $1.8B in the quarter, lifting cumulative FY26 orders toward $2B+, and the company exited Q2 with a record $5.9B AI backlog. Networking is now a structurally larger, higher-margin slice of revenue, and management pulled forward its long-term targets by "two years" — yet shares trade near 12x forward EPS, a fraction of the multiple awarded to pure-play AI names StockAnalysis· juin 2026.
