Verdict Express

B Bullish Tactical — 10-15 sessions, not a hold

PSIX makes engines and packaged power systems, including the gensets data centers buy for backup and prime power, and is majority-owned by China's Weichai. The stock collapsed 74% from its February high to $25.28 on July 29, then reclaimed 63% in seven sessions. The last leg was a +21% gap on Friday, off Thursday evening's Q2 print: revenue $152.5M against a $134M estimate, gross margin up 420bps sequentially to 27.1%, $30.8M of debt paid down in one quarter SEC EDGAR 8-K · Aug 2026. Set against that: a 7.3M-share float with 23.9% of it sold short, and a chart still below its 200-day average. This is a momentum-and-squeeze trade with defined risk, not a quality compounder. Our five-lens value board votes 4/5 bearish at this price.

Why Buy

  • Q2 revenue of $152.5M beat the $134M estimate by 14% and grew 18.6% over Q1, the sequential inflection the bulls were waiting for after two soft quarters.
  • Gross margin of 27.1% (up ~420bps from Q1) plus $30.8M of debt reduction in a single quarter, on a company carrying $168.8M of total debt.
  • Squeeze mechanics: 1.76M shares short (FINRA, July 15 settlement) on a 7.3M float, i.e. 23.9%, with 3.9 days to cover, and call OI stacked 3.5:1 over puts into August 21 expiry.
  • Management guides second-half sales roughly in line with H2 2025 as larger Power Systems orders enter production, materially stronger than the H1 run-rate, with data center demand called out as solid.
  • EV/EBITDA at 10.2x and trailing earnings around $3.00/share put the P/E near 13x: not deep value after the gap, but low enough that a durable margin repair re-rates it.

Why Avoid

  • The growth optics are still negative: revenue fell 21% year-over-year and EPS of $0.73 compares with $2.24 a year ago. The two 2026 quarters both missed a consensus that is only one or two analysts wide.
  • Price sits 26.2% above its own 20-day average after two unfilled gaps. A fill of Friday's gap alone is roughly -20% to $33 without invalidating anything fundamental.
  • Governance churn: third CEO since May (Xykis out, CFO Li interim, Richard Hu effective August 17), inside a company ~63% insider-held with Weichai in control. The tiny float cuts both ways.
  • Our deterministic value/quality board votes 4/5 bearish: operating margin 8.9%, gross margin 24.3% TTM, debt-to-equity 0.91, price 41% above its Graham number. Multi-method intrinsic valuation returned no verdict; the cash-flow detail it requires is not available with enough confidence, so we won't pretend to a fair value.

Business Overview

Power Solutions International (Wood Dale, Illinois, ~1,000 employees) designs and manufactures engines and integrated power systems: emission-certified engines, custom-engineered generator sets for standby and prime power, enclosures and electrification components. Its power systems serve data centers, oil & gas, microgrids, demand response and rental fleets; industrial products power forklifts and off-road equipment; a transportation line serves trucks and specialty vehicles. The company is majority-owned by Weichai America (insiders hold 63.2% overall), which leaves a public float of roughly 7.3M shares out of 23.1M outstanding. The 2026 story is a mix shift: larger Power Systems orders (data center gensets prominent among them) moving into production while the legacy industrial and transportation lines digest a soft first half.
