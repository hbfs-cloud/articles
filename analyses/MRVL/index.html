Marvell has become a concentrated AI data-infrastructure growth vehicle, with exceptional demand and a valuation that assumes flawless custom-silicon execution. Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue reached a record $2.739 billion, up 37% year over year; data-center revenue accelerated 46%. GAAP gross margin was 53.1% and GAAP diluted EPS $0.33, versus 58.9% and $0.94 on a non-GAAP basis. Q3 guidance of $3.15 billion plus or minus 5% and $1.10 plus or minus $0.05 non-GAAP EPS confirms acceleration. The AI transmission spans custom accelerators, PAM DSPs, Ethernet and optical connectivity, so Marvell can earn content both inside and around the XPU. The counterweight is 73.4x EV/EBITDA and 22.8x EV/revenue, customer concentration, $5.28 billion debt and Series A preferred dilution. The April 424B5 is senior-note debt, not a warrant. Business Overview

Marvell designs data-infrastructure semiconductors for data centers, enterprise networking, carriers, consumer and automotive/industrial markets. Its portfolio includes custom compute silicon, Ethernet switching, optical/PAM connectivity, storage controllers and PHYs.

The AI channel is broad: custom accelerators monetize hyperscaler silicon programs, while DSPs, switches and electro-optics move data between accelerators and racks. Q2 data-center revenue growth of 46% shows that connectivity and custom silicon are already reported revenue, not only a roadmap claim.

The model breaks if a large customer's custom program slips, customers internalize more design work, or foundry/substrate capacity constrains ramps. Data-center concentration, preferred-stock dilution and a 22.8x revenue multiple magnify program-level errors. Custom AI ASIC revenue arrives through long design cycles and customer-specific ramps, while electro-optics and storage remain cyclical. The model breaks if a hyperscaler slips a tape-out, insources silicon, or gross margin fails to recover as custom programs scale faster than higher-margin infrastructure products.
