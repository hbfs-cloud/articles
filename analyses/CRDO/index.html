Business Overview

Credo Technology designs high-speed connectivity solutions that move data inside AI and cloud data centers with maximum bandwidth per watt. Its flagship product family — Active Electrical Cables (AECs) — replaces passive copper and short-reach optics for in-rack and rack-to-rack links, while its optical DSPs, line-card retimers and SerDes IP address back-end accelerator fabrics. As hyperscalers scale GPU clusters, the connectivity bottleneck between thousands of accelerators is exactly where Credo sells Yahoo Finance· live.

The thesis is structural: AI rack architectures are exploding in port count and data rate (200G/400G/800G lanes), and Credo's energy-efficient AECs win on power and reliability versus optics for in-rack reach. The recent DustPhotonics acquisition (closed June 2026) pulls silicon-photonics design in-house, broadening the optical roadmap toward co-packaged optics. The flip side is concentration — a handful of hyperscalers drive the majority of revenue, so a single capex pause moves the model materially Insider Monkey· juin 2026.
