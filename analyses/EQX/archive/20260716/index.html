Business Overview

Equinox Gold is a mid-tier gold producer headquartered in Vancouver, operating mines across North America (Canada, USA, Mexico). The company produced a record 922,827 ounces in 2025 from a diversified portfolio anchored by the flagship Greenstone Mine in Ontario, Canada's newest large-scale gold mine.

In May 2026, Equinox announced a transformative all-share merger with Orla Mining (1.00 EQX per ORLA share), creating a senior gold producer with 1.1M oz annual production and a funded growth trajectory to 1.9M oz by 2030. The combined entity will have a pure North American footprint after the $1.1B Brazil divestiture in January 2026. The deal, backed by billionaire Pierre Lassonde, is expected to close Q3 2026. Both ISS and Glass Lewis recommend shareholders on each side vote FOR it, and the special meeting is set for July 22, 2026 (proxy deadline July 20).

Key assets include Greenstone (550K oz capacity, lowest AISC in portfolio), Los Filos (Mexico, legacy open-pit under optimization), Castle Mountain (Nevada), and post-merger additions of Orla's Camino Rojo and South Railroad projects providing organic growth without construction capex.

Thesis

Same value case, stricter discipline. EQX at 5.7x forward earnings and 1.22x book is cheaper than it was when we liked it at $9.79 — and the deal risk we were paid to wait through has essentially resolved, with the vote on July 22 and the arb spread under 1%. What changed is the metal: gold is in a confirmed 28% correction, and a 2.40-beta miner does not get to bottom before bullion does. So the trade moves from "scale in at spot" to "let it come to you": bids at $7.80–8.20 where the 2025 base, book value and a washed-out RSI converge. The alternative path for confirmation traders: a daily close back above $9.45 re-opens the long with a stop under $8.66. Between those two levels, the correct position is none.
