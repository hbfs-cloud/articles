Business Overview

Equinox Gold is a mid-tier gold producer headquartered in Vancouver, operating mines across North America (Canada, USA, Mexico). The company produced a record 922,827 ounces in 2025 from a diversified portfolio anchored by the flagship Greenstone Mine in Ontario (commercial production achieved 2024).

In May 2026, Equinox announced a transformative all-share merger with Orla Mining ($5.1B deal backed by Pierre Lassonde), creating a senior gold producer with 1.1M oz annual production and a funded growth trajectory to 1.9M oz. The combined entity has an implied market cap of ~$18.5B. The company's North American footprint was simplified in January 2026 when Brazil operations were sold and $1.1B in debt was retired.
