Business Overview

Ross Stores is the second-largest off-price apparel and home fashion retailer in the United States, operating roughly 2,200+ stores under the Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS banners. The model is simple and durable: buy brand-name and designer merchandise opportunistically at steep discounts, then sell it 20-60% below department-store prices in a no-frills, treasure-hunt format. That structural cost advantage means Ross actually benefits when the economy softens and shoppers hunt for value Finviz· live.

The flywheel is working under CEO Jim Conroy, with Q1 2026 revenue up ~21% YoY to a $23.8B TTM run-rate and earnings growth of +37%. Off-price excess inventory in the broader retail channel keeps Ross's buyers supplied with quality goods at attractive cost, while the chain's low price points insulate it from trade-down risk. The balance sheet carries $4.1B of cash against modest, mostly lease-related debt, funding a steady store-expansion runway and an aggressive capital-return program.
