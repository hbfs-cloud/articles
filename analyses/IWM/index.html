ETF Overview

IWM is the iShares Russell 2000 ETF, providing exposure to the US small-cap equity universe. With $75B in AUM and 2000+ holdings, it's the most widely-traded small-cap ETF, offering broad diversification across all sectors and industries of the US economy.

The Russell 2000 captures the smallest 2000 companies in the Russell 3000 index, representing approximately 7% of total US equity market capitalization. Small-caps tend to outperform during economic expansions and rate-cutting cycles, as these companies carry more floating-rate debt and benefit from improved borrowing conditions. The index is more domestically focused than large-cap indices, making it a pure play on the US economy.
