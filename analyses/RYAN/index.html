Business Overview

Ryan Specialty Holdings is a leading specialty insurance brokerage firm, providing wholesale distribution, underwriting management, and specialty program management services. The company focuses on complex and hard-to-place insurance risks that require specialized expertise.

Founded by Pat Ryan (also founder of Aon), the company operates through three segments: Wholesale Brokerage, Binding Authority, and Underwriting Management. Revenue has grown to $3.1B with 15.8% growth, driven by a hard insurance pricing environment that favors specialty brokers. The company's 41.8% gross margin and 22.8% ROE demonstrate strong unit economics. RYAN has been an active consolidator in the fragmented specialty insurance market.
