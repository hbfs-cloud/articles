Verdict Express

A- Neutral Moderate confidence

LPL Financial is a scaled wealth-management platform whose economics depend on advisor assets, organic net new assets, payout discipline and the yield earned on client cash. Q2 2026 was strong: GAAP net income rose 39% to $379 million, adjusted EPS rose 29% to $5.84, gross profit increased 24% to $1.62 billion and total client assets reached $2.6 trillion. The mix improved as advisory assets grew 46% to $1.5 trillion and reached 60.4% of total assets. The caveat is that organic net new assets were only $23 billion, or 4% annualized, while client cash fell sequentially to $57 billion. Commonwealth integration is the next execution test: management still targets a Q4 2026 conversion and about 90% asset retention. The A- grade reflects durable scale and profitable growth, not a live recommendation. The preserved $362.97/$352.05/$369.98/$379.00 plan is rejected because it pays only 0.64R to TP1 and 1.47R to TP2.

Why Buy

  • Q2 total client assets reached $2.6T, up 34% YoY; advisory assets rose 46% to $1.5T.
  • Advisory assets increased to 60.4% of total assets from 55.3%, improving the recurring-fee mix.
  • Q2 adjusted EPS was a record $5.84, up 29% YoY; GAAP EPS was $4.74, up 39%.
  • Recruited assets were $25B in Q2 and $89B over the trailing twelve months.
  • Commonwealth run-rate EBITDA is now estimated at $435M, with approximately 90% asset retention still expected.

Why Avoid

  • Organic net new assets were $23B, only 4% annualized despite the much larger asset base.
  • Client cash fell $2B sequentially to $57B and declined to 2.2% of client assets from 2.5% in Q1.
  • Commonwealth conversion is not complete; the Q4 timetable and 90% retention assumption remain execution risks.
  • Core G&A is still guided to $2.140B-$2.165B for 2026, including Commonwealth costs.
  • At $365.50, a simple annualized Q2 adjusted-EPS proxy is about 15.6x; that proxy can overstate normalized earnings if cash yields or markets weaken.

Business Overview

LPL earns primarily from advisory fees and commissions generated on a large advisor-led asset base, plus transaction activity and the spread on client cash. Q2 2026 total client assets were $2.6 trillion, including $1.5 trillion of advisory assets; the shift toward advisory assets makes revenue more recurring but still market-sensitive.

The technology and AI transmission channel is operational, not an AI-product multiple: better advisor workflows, data, compliance and portfolio tools can improve recruiting, asset retention and advisor productivity. The economic proof is recruited assets, organic net new assets, gross profit and controlled core G&A, not the number of AI announcements.

The model breaks if markets reduce fee-bearing assets, clients reallocate cash away from sweep programs, advisor recruiting slows, or Commonwealth conversion causes attrition and cost overruns. Interest-rate changes can also move cash-sweep economics faster than headline client assets and reported growth suggest.

Dilution Risk

LPL ended Q2 with $430M of corporate cash and a 1.91x leverage ratio. It repurchased $309M of stock during the quarter, plans roughly $300M for Q3, and the board added $2.5B to the repurchase authorization on July 23. The current SEC review found ordinary equity compensation and repurchase activity but no reviewed prospectus supplement establishing an ATM. The relevant capital-allocation tension is therefore not micro-cap dilution; it is balancing buybacks and dividends against Commonwealth integration, advisor succession financing and $7.79B of reported debt. LPL does not provide a current fully diluted point-in-time bridge in the reviewed materials, so the dossier does not construct a synthetic total.
