Business Overview

DraftKings is the leading digital sports entertainment and gaming company in the United States, operating in online sports betting (OSB) and iGaming across 25+ states. The platform combines real-money sports wagering with daily fantasy sports and an expanding online casino product.

Revenue has grown to $6.3B TTM driven by geographic expansion and increasing customer lifetime value. The company's proprietary technology stack (migrated from SBTech) enables faster feature deployment and better margins. With iGaming legalization advancing in multiple states, the total addressable market continues to expand. Q1 2026 marked the first quarter of sustained profitability, a critical milestone that validates the path to profitable scale.
