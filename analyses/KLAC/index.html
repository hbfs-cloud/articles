Business Overview

KLA Corporation is the global leader in semiconductor process control and yield management, providing inspection, metrology, and data analytics systems used by every major chipmaker. KLA's tools detect defects and measure critical dimensions at each stage of chip manufacturing — ensuring quality as feature sizes shrink to atomic scale.

With >50% market share in process control, KLA benefits from a critical insight: as chip complexity increases (more layers, smaller features, new materials), the number of required inspection steps per wafer grows exponentially. The transition to 2nm and High-NA EUV lithography requires significantly more KLA tools per fab — creating a structural growth driver beyond the traditional capex cycle.
