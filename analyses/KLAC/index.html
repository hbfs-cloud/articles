Business Overview

KLA sells inspection, metrology and process-control systems used by logic, foundry, memory and advanced-packaging manufacturers, then earns service revenue from the installed base. Semiconductor Process Control generated $3.257B of Q4 FY2026 revenue, while Specialty Semiconductor Process and PCB/Component Inspection contributed $160M and $241M.

The AI transmission channel is process intensity rather than GPU unit volume alone. Leading-edge logic, HBM and advanced packaging add layers, interconnects and yield-sensitive steps; each additional defect opportunity raises the economic value of inspection and metrology before a wafer or package can ship.

The model breaks if customers defer fab-equipment purchases, export controls remove accessible China demand, or competing process-control techniques reduce KLA content per wafer. Service cushions volatility, but customer procurement schedules, order pushouts and a premium valuation can still make earnings and the share price cyclical. Recurring service revenue reached $3.126B in FY2026, moderating but not eliminating wafer-fab-equipment cyclicality. The operating test is whether inspection intensity continues rising faster than wafer starts while export restrictions leave the company able to serve installed tools and collect cash.
