ETF Overview

EWJ is the iShares MSCI Japan ETF, providing exposure to large and mid-cap Japanese equities. With $14B in AUM and ~230 holdings, it captures ~85% of the Japanese equity market across all sectors including auto (Toyota, Denso), electronics (Sony, Keyence), financials (MUFG), and technology (Tokyo Electron, SoftBank).

Japan's equity market has undergone a structural transformation driven by corporate governance reforms (Tokyo Stock Exchange's push for ROE improvement), BOJ monetary policy normalization, and Warren Buffett's high-profile accumulation of Japanese trading companies. The weak yen has boosted export competitiveness and earnings translation for Japan's globally-oriented companies. EWJ provides APAC diversification and exposure to one of the strongest developed market equity stories.
