Business Overview

CECO Environmental provides critical environmental, energy, and fluid handling solutions. The company operates across Engineered Systems (industrial air quality, pollution control, fluid handling) and Industrial Process Solutions (thermal energy management, dampers, expansion joints).

CECO has emerged as a key beneficiary of the AI data center buildout, providing cooling and air quality systems for hyperscale data centers. On June 1, 2026, the company completed its transformative $2.2B acquisition of Thermon Group Holdings, issuing approximately 22.5M new CECO shares (~39% dilution to pre-deal shareholders) plus ~$330M in cash funded via its credit facility. The combined entity targets $1.275-1.375B in 2026 revenue and $40M+ in cost synergies. An active S-3ASR automatic shelf registration (filed 2025) is on file, giving CECO the ability to issue additional securities at any time.
