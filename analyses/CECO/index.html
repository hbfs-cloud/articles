Business Overview

CECO Environmental provides critical environmental, energy, and fluid handling solutions. The company operates across Engineered Systems (industrial air quality, pollution control, fluid handling) and Industrial Process Solutions (thermal energy management, dampers, expansion joints).

CECO has emerged as a key beneficiary of the AI data center buildout, providing cooling and air quality systems for hyperscale data centers. The company's environmental compliance solutions also benefit from tightening global emissions regulations. The Q1 2026 earnings explosion signals a fundamental inflection point in the company's growth trajectory.
