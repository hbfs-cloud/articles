Company Overview

Atlassian (TEAM) is a technology company from Australia that builds software tools for teams to work together better. They are famous for products like Jira (for managing projects), Confluence (for sharing documents and knowledge), and Trello (for personal productivity). They also offer solutions for IT teams and even AI tools like Rovo to help teams be more productive. Atlassian aims to help all kinds of teams collaborate smoothly and effectively.

Key Products & Recent Developments

  • Jira: Project management platform for planning and tracking work.
  • Confluence: Workspace for creating and sharing team knowledge.
  • Loom: A tool for asynchronous video communication (acquired).
  • Rovo: An AI offering that assists teams with Search, Chat, and Agent capabilities.
  • Recent Acquisitions: The Browser Company of New York Inc. (BCNY) and A Software Company (DX), integrating new technologies for enterprise browsers and engineering intelligence.
  • Cloud-First Strategy: Atlassian is moving away from on-premises Data Center offerings, focusing on cloud-based subscriptions. This transition is a key focus for the company and its investors.

New CFO Appointment (Simply Wall St) February 24, 2026

Atlassian has appointed James Chuong, former LinkedIn CFO, as its new Chief Financial Officer. This is a significant leadership change that could influence the company's financial strategies, investment in AI products, and capital allocation. Stock Down 76% but Analysts Still Say Buy (24/7 Wall St.) February 24, 2026

Despite Atlassian's stock falling significantly (down 76% over the past year), 25 out of 33 analysts still rate it as a 'Buy' or 'Strong Buy'. The consensus price target is $177.85, much higher than the current price. This suggests analysts see long-term value, even with concerns about cloud growth deceleration and a shareholder investigation. 24/7 Wall St.

Why Atlassian Stock Just Popped (Motley Fool) February 24, 2026

Atlassian shares recently bounced back after a Jefferies analyst stated that "Software will survive" and Atlassian is well-positioned to benefit from the increasing need for IT collaboration due to AI-generated code. The analyst sees Atlassian growing 20% annually and recommends it as a 'buy'. This optimism comes despite a 73% loss in value over the past year. Motley Fool

Key Risks for Atlassian

Every investment has risks. For Atlassian, understanding these helps you make informed decisions.
  • Competition: Atlassian operates in a competitive market with big players like Microsoft, ServiceNow, and GitLab.
  • Cloud Transition: The shift from Data Center to Cloud offerings is complex and if not managed well, could impact growth.
  • AI Disruption: While Atlassian is integrating AI, rapid advancements in AI could also disrupt their core products or reduce demand for certain software features.
  • Valuation: Despite the recent stock drop, some metrics might still appear high, reflecting growth expectations.
  • Shareholder Investigation: Ongoing investigation into potential securities issues adds uncertainty.
