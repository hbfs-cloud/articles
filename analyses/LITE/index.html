Why Lumentum matters for AI: Every AI training cluster and inference farm requires massive bandwidth between GPUs, switches, and storage. Optical transceivers convert electrical signals to light for high-speed data transfer over fiber. As clusters scale from thousands to millions of GPUs, the demand for 800G and upcoming 1.6T transceivers is exploding. Lumentum's vertically integrated photonic chip manufacturing (from InP wafers to packaged modules) gives it a structural advantage over competitors who rely on external chip sourcing. The company is a critical Tier-1 supplier to all major hyperscalers building out AI infrastructure.

What 113% institutional ownership means: When institutional ownership exceeds 100%, it typically means shares are being lent for short selling. Institutions own the shares (long), but those shares are simultaneously borrowed and sold short by other parties. This creates a dual claim on the same shares. With 16.9% short interest, this is entirely consistent — institutions are lending their shares to shorts, collecting borrow fees while maintaining their long positions. This dynamic creates squeeze potential: if shorts need to cover rapidly, there are no free shares available in the lending pool, forcing them to buy in the open market at escalating prices.
