Subject: The Complete AI Data Center Platform: Lumentum to Join Forces with Marvell

[Dear NAME / INSERT CUSTOMARY GREETING],

I am writing to share exciting news with you. We announced today that Lumentum has agreed to combine with Marvell Technology, creating a category defining company to address the market needs for AI infrastructure connectivity in next generation data centers.

Marvell’s strengths in custom silicon, switching, and connectivity complement Lumentum’s expertise in optical and photonic technologies. The combined company will offer a broad portfolio of innovative products that will allow us to serve our customers across the entire AI network fabric.

From the existing scale-across and scale-out markets, to future scale-up and scale-in markets, the benefit to our customers is our ability to accelerate time to market with integrated, high-performance solutions optimized at system level.

The transaction also creates a company with significant advantages including greater resources behind our roadmaps, extreme co-design capabilities tailored to customer requirements, and the ability to build our solutions at scale.

In terms of next steps, we expect the transaction to close in approximately 12-18 months, subject to customary closing conditions, including approval by Lumentum and Marvell shareholders and the required regulatory approvals.

Until the transaction closes, it is business as usual. Lumentum and Marvell remain separate public companies and will continue to operate independently. Your existing agreements, purchase orders, payment processes, points of contact, and day-to-day interactions with Lumentum will remain unchanged. We remain fully committed to our partnership and making this a seamless combination for you.

We value your partnership and appreciate your continued collaboration. We look forward to building on our relationship and, subject to closing, bringing together capabilities that can help customers meet the demands of an increasingly connected and compute-intensive world.

On behalf of everyone at Lumentum, thank you for your continued partnership. As always, if you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me or Adrian Benjamin.

Sincerely,

Matthias Van Broekhoven

EVP, Business Operations

This communication relates to a proposed transaction between Marvell Technology Inc. (“Marvell”) and Lumentum (the “Transaction”). In connection with the Transaction, Marvell intends to file with the SEC a registration statement on Form S-4 (the “Form S-4”) that will include a joint proxy statement for Marvell’s and Lumentum’s respective stockholders and that will also constitute a prospectus with respect to the shares of Marvell’s common stock to be issued in the Transaction (the “Joint Proxy Statement/Prospectus”).
