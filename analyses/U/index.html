Business Overview

Unity Software operates the world's most widely-used real-time 3D development platform, powering over 70% of the world's top mobile games and expanding rapidly into industrial applications, automotive visualization, and digital twins. The Unity Engine is the creation tool; Unity Runtime powers the experiences across 20+ platforms.

The company generates $1.9B in annual revenue from two segments: Create Solutions (engine licensing, AI tools, cloud services) and Grow Solutions (advertising, monetization, analytics for game developers). After the 2024 runtime fee controversy that caused significant developer backlash, Unity has restructured its pricing, replaced its CEO, and refocused on platform stability. AI-powered tools (Unity Muse for natural language game creation, Sentis for in-game ML) represent the next growth vector.
