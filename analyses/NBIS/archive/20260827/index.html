Verdict Express

C+ Neutral Moderate confidence

Nebius has the strongest operating momentum among the neocloud cohort, but its valuation and financing cadence assume that exceptional Q2 unit economics survive a brutal capacity ramp. Q2 2026 revenue rose 454% to $582.3 million; AI cloud supplied $574.9 million, or 98% of group revenue, and the segment delivered a 50% adjusted EBITDA margin. Annualized run-rate revenue reached $3.0 billion, while management reported more than $9 billion of expected 2026 customer prepayments. The June 30 legal denominator is 271.855 million Class A plus Class B shares, not the 238.4 million Class A figure. At $209.7599, that implies about $57.03 billion of equity value and $59.19 billion of enterprise value after $10.20 billion debt and $8.04 billion cash, or roughly 43.5x trailing revenue and 229x EBITDA. Pre-funded warrants, awards and gross convert claims sit outside that legal count. The C+ grade recognizes growth and dilution. The unchanged $214.28 plan is rejected because TP1 offers only 1.23R.

Business Overview

Nebius sells GPU cloud capacity and an AI-native software platform, with AI cloud accounting for nearly all current group revenue. Sales are driven by deployed GPU clusters, customer utilization, contract pricing and the pace at which new capacity becomes available.

The AI transmission channel is direct: model builders and enterprises reserve accelerators, often prepaying to secure capacity; Nebius uses those commitments to finance GPUs and datacenters, then recognizes cloud revenue as service is delivered. Q2 annualized run-rate revenue of $3.0B and four billion-dollar-scale contracts demonstrate this loop.

The model breaks if GPU pricing compresses, customer prepayments reverse into delivery disputes, power or equipment arrives late, or capital raised through converts and equity fails to produce adequate returns. The broader group offers optionality, but 98% AI-cloud revenue means the current equity thesis is concentrated. The decisive evidence will be recognized revenue, deployed megawatts and cash payback after financing costs, not contract count or prepayment inflows viewed independently.

Dilution Risk

At June 30 Nebius had 238,400,165 Class A and 33,455,053 Class B shares outstanding, or a 271,855,218-share legal denominator; Class C was nil. The 12,729,493 ATM shares sold at a $223.60 average were delivered from treasury and are included in that count, while 12,270,507 program shares remained unsold. Outside the legal count, 21,065,936 NVIDIA pre-funded warrants are treated as outstanding for EPS, 10,043,254 awards were anti-dilutive in Q2, and 65,416,804 gross if-converted shares were excluded from diluted EPS. Adding those observable claims yields a 368.4M sensitivity, not a fully diluted forecast, because convert settlement and capped calls can change actual issuance. At $209.7599, the legal count implies $57.03B market capitalization and approximately $59.19B enterprise value after $10.20B debt and $8.04B cash.
