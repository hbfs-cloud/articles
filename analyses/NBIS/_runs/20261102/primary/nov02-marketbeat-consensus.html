About Nebius Group

Nebius Group (NASDAQ:NBIS) is a technology company focused on providing cloud infrastructure and services for artificial intelligence and machine learning applications. Through its Nebius AI division, the company offers access to high-performance computing, graphics processing units (GPUs), storage, networking, and related tools designed to help businesses and developers train, deploy, and operate AI models.

The company also operates or supports other technology businesses, including Toloka, an AI data and evaluation platform; TripleTen, an online education provider focused on technology careers; and Avride, a developer of autonomous driving and delivery technologies. Its services are aimed at customers in multiple international markets, with infrastructure and operations serving businesses, developers, and technology organizations outside Russia.

Nebius Group was formed through the restructuring and rebranding of Yandex N.V., the former parent company of the Yandex technology group. In 2024, Yandex N.V. completed the divestment of its Russian businesses and adopted the Nebius Group name. The company is headquartered in Amsterdam, and its founder and chief executive officer is Arkady Volozh.
