Verdict Express

D Bullish Moderate confidence

CleanSpark remains a bitcoin miner with an undeveloped data-center option, and the current D grade correctly refuses to capitalize that option as if it were contracted AI revenue. Fiscal Q3 revenue fell 30.5% YoY to $138.0 million, while GAAP net loss reached $239.8 million and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA was -$113.0 million. The quarter included $116.3 million of bitcoin fair-value losses, but core pressure is also visible in $85.5 million of cash cost of revenue against $138.0 million of mining revenue and $111.0 million of depreciation. Liquidity is not negligible: $202.6 million cash, $814.9 million of bitcoin and $761 million working capital. The counterweight is $1.78 billion of long-term debt and only about $0.8 billion of equity. Sandersville and other power sites could be converted for AI/HPC, but no customer, lease revenue or financed conversion appears in the quarter. Publication should therefore frame CLSK as a leveraged miner with asset reuse potential, not an AI landlord. The $12.66 activation remains mandatory.

Business Overview

CleanSpark currently earns substantially all reported revenue from bitcoin mining. BTC produced and realized price must cover electricity, hosting, fleet maintenance and heavy equipment depreciation; fiscal Q3 mining revenue was $138.0M against $85.5M cash cost of revenue before $111.0M depreciation. Cash and BTC totaled about $1.02B, still below $1.78B of long-term debt.

The AI transmission channel is reuse of powered land, interconnections and data-center shells for HPC. Sandersville is an option to lease or convert capacity, but the latest quarter reports no AI tenant, contracted rent or AI segment revenue. Conversion also requires cooling, network and reliability investment beyond mining infrastructure.

The model breaks if hashprice remains below fully loaded mining cost, BTC treasury value falls against debt, or HPC conversion demands capital before a bankable tenant is secured. Site megawatts do not guarantee suitable power quality or returns, and reporting-control weakness makes rapid project financing harder to evaluate.
