Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

On October 23, 2026, Hyperliquid Strategies Inc (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that its board of directors approved an increase to the Company’s previously announced repurchase program from $30 million to up to $100 million of the Company’s outstanding shares of common stock, and an extension of the program through October 31, 2027. As of October 23, 2026, the prior authorization from December 2025 had approximately $2 million remaining, leaving approximately $72 million available under the increased authorization.

 

There is no guarantee as to the number of shares that will be repurchased under the amended program, and the repurchase program may be extended, suspended or discontinued at any time without prior notice at the Company’s discretion. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
