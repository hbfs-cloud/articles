Business Overview

GE Aerospace is the world's largest jet engine manufacturer, spun off from General Electric in April 2024 as a pure-play aerospace company. The company supplies engines for both commercial aviation (LEAP for 737 MAX/A320neo, GE9X for 777X, GEnx) and defense/propulsion (F110, T700 for military applications).

The business benefits from a massive installed base of 44,000+ engines generating recurring aftermarket revenue that accounts for over 60% of total revenue. Post-COVID aviation recovery combined with defense spending tailwinds create a dual growth engine. Revenue split is approximately 70% commercial aviation and 30% defense/propulsion technologies.
