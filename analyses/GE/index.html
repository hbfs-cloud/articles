Business Overview

GE Aerospace is the world's leading designer and manufacturer of jet engines for commercial and military aircraft, built around its Commercial Engines & Services (CES) segment and its Defense & Propulsion Technologies (DPT) segment. Its CFM International joint venture with Safran produces the LEAP engine — the dominant powerplant on the Boeing 737 MAX and Airbus A320neo families — while the GEnx and GE9X power widebodies. The real economic engine is the aftermarket: every unit sold locks in decades of high-margin spare parts, overhaul and service-agreement revenue. GE Aerospace IR· 2025

Following the 2024 spin-offs of GE HealthCare and GE Vernova, the company is now a focused pure-play aerospace name with a record backlog and structural pricing power as a large narrowbody fleet flies more hours and ages into peak-shop-visit windows. Recent newsflow has highlighted GE's AI-led engineering and even space-vehicle propulsion work, expanding the long-term addressable market beyond traditional aviation. Simply Wall St· juin 2026
