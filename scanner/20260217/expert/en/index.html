Methodology

Multi-Factor Approach

The algorithmic scanner combines 4 quantitative dimensions to generate a composite score from 0 to 100:

  • Technical (0-100): Price pattern analysis (support/resistance, breakouts, convergences), momentum indicators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic), and moving averages (EMA 20/50/200 alignment).
  • Volume (0-100): Institutional buyer/seller flows (OBV, relative volume vs 20-day average), accumulation/distribution presence, and price movement confirmation by volume.
  • Momentum (0-100): Trend strength (ADX, Rate of Change), absence of bearish divergences, and directional indicator alignment.
  • Risk (0-100): Risk/reward ratio (R/R), stop-loss size, implied volatility (ATR), and distance to invalidation levels.

Setups are then classified according to 3 dominant strategies:

  • Momentum Expansion: Strong bullish trend continuation (RSI > 50, positive MACD, increasing volume).
  • Breakout Squeeze: Major resistance breakout with exceptional volume (> +80% vs 20-day average).
  • Pre-Squeeze: Consolidation on key support before bullish squeeze (RSI 40-50, lateral volume).

Market Regime: Early Risk-Off

The Early Risk-Off regime is detected via 6 macro components:

  1. VIX: Crossing the 18 threshold (rising volatility).
  2. S&P 500: Stabilization after key support break (6870).
  3. DXY (Dollar Index): Bearish trend (< 97), favoring international assets.
  4. Gold (XAU): Structural rally (> $460), safe haven demand.
  5. Credit Spreads: Stability (no credit panic).
  6. Liquidity: Moderate contraction (flows to defensives).

In this regime, Momentum Expansion strategies (50%) dominate to capture strong trends, Breakout Squeeze strategies (30%) exploit volume breakouts, and Pre-Squeeze strategies (20%) anticipate technical bounces. Geographic diversification (50% US, 50% International) is essential to mitigate US systematic risk.
