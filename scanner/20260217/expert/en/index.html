Methodology

The MarketWatch algorithmic scanner uses a multi-factor quantitative approach to detect A+ ultra-safe setups in real-time. The process relies on 5 pillars:

1. Market Regime Detection

The algorithm analyzes 6 macro components (S&P 500, VIX, DXY, TLT, Credit, Gold) and 18 recent news articles via NLP to classify the regime into 5 categories: RiskOn (bullish, favors Momentum Expansion), Neutral (balanced), EarlyRiskOff (rising volatility, favors Pre-Squeeze), RiskOff (pure defensive), Recovery (post-crash, favors Breakout Squeeze). The detected regime automatically adjusts strategy weights.

2. Multi-Strategy Screening

Three complementary DSL strategies are executed in parallel:

  • Momentum Expansion: Confirmed breakouts with institutional volume, RSI >50 without divergence, MACD golden cross, aligned moving averages. Weight: 40% in EARLY RISK-OFF.
  • Breakout Squeeze: Volatility compressions (Bollinger squeeze), dry accumulation volume, low ATR, nearby key resistance. Weight: 30%.
  • Pre-Squeeze: Technical oversolds (RSI <50), bullish MACD divergences, major support, declining selling volume. Weight: 30%.

3. Composite Scoring (4 Factors)

Each setup is scored out of 100 points via 4 sub-scores:

  • Technical (25 pts): Pattern quality (breakout, divergence, compression), moving average alignment, RSI/MACD/ATR indicators.
  • Volume (25 pts): Relative volume vs 20-day average, OBV (On Balance Volume), institutional flows, bid/ask distribution.
  • Momentum (25 pts): Trend strength (ADX), EMA 20 slope, price velocity, absence of bearish divergences.
  • Risk (25 pts): Historical volatility (beta, ATR), technical stop loss, R/R ratio, liquidity (bid/ask spread).

4. Ultra-Safe Filters

To guarantee optimal risk profile, A+ setups must satisfy:

  • Composite score >87/100 (institutional threshold)
  • R/R >1:2 (minimum 1:2 risk/reward)
  • Deep liquidity (average volume >2M shares/day or AUM >$10B for ETFs)
  • Cap >$200B or AUM >$10B (mega-caps only)
  • Dividend >1.5% or defensive status (Healthcare, Consumer Staples, Utilities, leading Semis, Safe Havens)
  • Beta <1.0 (moderate volatility, correction resilience)

5. Backtest Validation

Each setup is backtested over 6 periods (1 year, 6 months, 3 months, 1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week) to validate historical robustness. Key metrics include: Win Rate (>65% required), Max Drawdown (<15% required), Sharpe Ratio (>1.0 required), R-Squared (>0.70 for consistency). Only setups with solid backtest are retained.
