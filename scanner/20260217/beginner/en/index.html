How This Scanner Works

Computer Algorithm Finds Good Stocks

This scanner is like a robot that looks at thousands of stocks every day and finds the best opportunities. It checks 4 main things for each stock:

  • Price Patterns - Is the stock bouncing off support? Breaking through resistance? Going up steadily?
  • Buying Interest - Are more people buying than usual? Is there heavy volume?
  • Momentum - Is the trend strong? Are things getting better or worse?
  • Risk Level - How far could it fall if things go wrong? Is the risk worth the potential reward?

Each stock gets a score from 0 to 10. Stocks rated 8.5 or higher make the list - those are high quality setups!

Three Types of Opportunities

The scanner found 3 types of good opportunities today:

  • Strong Uptrends (50%) - Stocks that are steadily going up. Like climbing stairs - one step at a time, but clearly headed higher. Examples: AAPL, GLD, TSM.
  • Breaking Higher (30%) - Stocks that just broke through a ceiling (resistance) to reach new heights. Like a rocket breaking through clouds. Examples: ASML, ABBV, EWJ.
  • Bouncing Back (20%) - Stocks that fell too much and are now recovering. Like a rubber ball bouncing off the floor. Examples: ABT, ABNB.

Why Half US and Half International?

We picked 5 US stocks and 5 international (Europe, Asia, Gold) to spread out risk. Think of it like not putting all your eggs in one basket. If American markets have a bad day, European or Asian markets might do fine. This is called diversification - it's the number one rule of smart investing. Also, the US dollar is weak right now, which is actually good for international stocks!

Where the Data Comes From

This scanner uses real market data from trusted sources like Yahoo Finance, NASDAQ, and other professional financial databases.
