Lessons & Takeaways

Regime shift was the defining factor: The RISK-ON → RECOVERY pivot mid-week (confirmed Thursday, VIX trending up) invalidated the bullish expansion thesis underpinning Breakout setups opened Monday–Wednesday. INTC (-16%), MU (-9%), GLW (-7.6%) were all Breakout entries from the worst session (05/12) — compounded by the semi-specific macro rotation out of memory/connectivity names.

What worked — Momentum dominates: PANW (+17.8%) and CRWD (+13.3%) both Momentum from 05/11, cleared TP1 with conviction. TTE repeated across 3 scans (Mon/Tue/Wed) and stayed trending +1.4% — low volatility, reliable carry. XLE and LLY Momentum also holding well.

What didn't — Breakout 0% HR on resolved: 6 of 8 stops were Breakout setups. Pattern: high-entry on expansion day → regime fades → price retreats through stop. Adjustment for next week: reduce Breakout allocation when regime confidence <70 or trending down; require volume confirmation above 1.5× 20-day avg.

Notable recurring winner — FTNT: Appeared twice (05/12 at $115.5, 05/13 at $113) and is trending +6.3% / +8.6% respectively — approaching TP1 on both entries. Best setup of the week on a risk-adjusted basis.
