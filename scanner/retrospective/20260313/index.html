Lessons & Adjustments

The Week in One Sentence

The scanner correctly identified the regime shift to Risk-Off and adapted its picks in real time — but one impulsive intraday special scan (VIX Deflation, March 10 09:30) plus two systematic errors (gold miners in stagflation; SaaS in front of earnings in Risk-Off) accounted for 10 of the 12 confirmed stops this week.

✅ What Worked

Defensive consumer staples (KR, WMT, ADM) — 3/3 positive, 2 TP1

In stagflation (high VIX + hot CPI), consumer staples with pricing power are the cleanest Risk-Off play. This should be the first allocation in any Risk-Off configuration. Minimum 2 staples setups in every Risk-Off scan.

Short squeeze plays are macro-independent — always include 1+

HIMS TP1 (+13.9%) despite Early Risk-Off because squeeze dynamics are idiosyncratic. Driven by positioning and catalysts, not SPX direction. This category should always have at least 1 representative per scan.

Risk-Off algorithm adaptation on March 12 was textbook-perfect

0 stops in 1 day, 1 instant TP1 (GOLD/Barrick), all energy/defense setups positive MtM. Energy (COP, DVN) + Defense (RTX) + Commodity (GOLD) + Staples (WMT) + Inverse hedge (SH) — this is the correct Risk-Off template.

❌ What Failed

VIX Deflation special scans — DISCONTINUED

VIX intraday compression is volatility noise, not a regime change. Triggered on a single-session VIX dip while macro drivers were all still intact. Result: 0% HR, 6 stops. This scan type is permanently discontinued. New condition: VIX must close below 22 for 2 consecutive sessions before any risk-on repositioning.

Gold miners (NEM, GDX) ≠ gold commodity in stagflation

Miners underperform physical gold when: (1) energy costs spike (CPI), (2) VIX > 25 (miners classified as risk assets), (3) geopolitical uncertainty creates operational risk. New rule: in Risk-Off with CPI > 2.5%, replace GDX/NEM with GLD, DBA, or Barrick (GOLD) individually.

No SaaS/tech within 5 days of earnings in Risk-Off regime

ADBE had earnings March 12 and was in the March 9 scan. SaaS names get double pressure pre-earnings in Risk-Off: macro multiple compression + earnings risk premium. New filter: exclude tickers with earnings within 5 calendar days if regime is Risk-Off or Early Risk-Off.

TLT is not a safe haven when CPI is the risk driver

TLT stopped out March 9, near-stop again March 13. Long-duration bonds are the epicenter of the problem in stagflation (not a safe haven). Correct safe havens in inflation Risk-Off: GLD, DBA, commodity producers, defensive staples. TLT only belongs in deflationary Risk-Off (growth shock, banking crisis).
