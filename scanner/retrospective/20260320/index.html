Lessons & Adjustments

The VIX Deflation Trap

The Mar 10 scan was a process failure. Attempting to play a VIX mean-reversion with 14 tech/growth setups (AMD, NVDA, ASML, PLTR) while the macro regime was clearly flipping to "Early Risk-Off" resulted in 8 stops. Adjustment: In regimes where VIX > 25, growth momentum is suspended. Scans must pivot 100% to Defensives and Commodities until VIX < 20.

Speculative Small-Cap Ban

The Mar 18 scan proved that speculative beta (ANTX, DWSN, BTDR) has zero resilience in high-volatility environments. We are implementing a hard filter: minimum Market Cap $2B for any Momentum setup during "Risk-Off" or "Early Risk-Off" regimes. No more penny stock momentum in panic weeks.

Energy/Agri Over-Weight

While the tech scan failed, the Energy/Commodity scans (Mar 12, 17, 19) are significantly outperforming. AGRO, HAL, CHCI, DVN, and FANG are keeping the portfolio afloat. Future scans will prioritize these inflation-hedge sectors with a 2x weight multiplier during credit-stress regimes.

Patience on Resolution

The 5.9% HR is an artifact of timing. Stops hit fast in panics; targets take time to reach in trending rotations. We are marking this retrospective as **HEAVILY PROVISIONAL**. We will review these 57 open positions in the next cycle to see if the Energy thesis delivers the expected alpha.
