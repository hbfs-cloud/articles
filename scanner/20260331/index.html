Methodology

1. Market Regime Detection

The regime is determined by 6 macro components: VIX (volatility), SPX (equity trend), DXY (dollar strength), TLT (bond flight), Credit (HYG spreads), and Liquidity. Each component is scored 0–1 and weighted to produce a composite regime score. Today’s score of 0.28 with VIX at 1.00 (maximum fear) confirms Early Risk-Off. The regime drives strategy allocation: Pullback 50%, Momentum 30%, Breakout 20%.

2. Multi-Strategy Screening

Three complementary DSL-based screens run in parallel: (1) Oversold bounce: RSI14 < 35 and volume > 1.5x average — catching capitulation; (2) Momentum expansion: price above SMA20, volume > 2x average, RSI 50–75 — riding strong trends; (3) Breakout squeeze: price above SMA50, ATR14 > 1.2x ATR28 — volatility expansion. Short Squeeze is excluded per policy (since March 20, 2026). Only 4 strategy labels are used: Momentum, Breakout, Pullback, Pre-Squeeze.

3. Composite Scoring (4 Factors)

Each candidate is scored 0–100 across 4 factors: Technical (RSI, MACD, moving averages, support/resistance), Volume (relative volume, accumulation/distribution), Momentum (rate of change, trend strength), and Risk (ATR-adjusted stop proximity, R/R ratio). The composite is the weighted average, with minimum threshold of 85 for inclusion.

4. A+ Selection Criteria

From 100+ screened candidates, only 10 make the final cut. Requirements: (1) Score ≥ 85; (2) ≥ 3 confluent technical signals; (3) identifiable catalyst; (4) sufficient liquidity (>$10M daily volume for stocks, >$50M for ETFs); (5) no active dilution risk (SEC S-3/S-1 check); (6) not in open portfolio positions; (7) geographic diversification (min 5 US + 2 EU + 1 APAC + 2 ETFs).

5. Validation & Ranking

Final validation includes: anti-duplicate position check (18 tickers excluded from open portfolio), 70% minimum novelty vs. previous scan (90% achieved today with only EQNR repeated), dilution screening for small/mid-caps, and insider transaction analysis. Picks are ranked by composite score and presented with full trade plans including entry range, stop loss, TP1, TP2, R/R ratio, and time horizon.
