You can have up to 15 trades open at the same time. The executor uses a 3-phase entry window (9:30–12:00 ET / 15:30–18:00 Paris): it tries to fill near entry in Phase 1, relaxes conditions in Phase 2, and places a market order in Phase 3 if price is still close — otherwise skips the signal.
3

4
When the price hits TP1: sell 50% of your shares to lock in profit, and let the remaining 50% run toward TP2. Consider manually moving stop to entry to eliminate downside risk on the remaining shares.
▶
VWAP Gate (built into the 3-phase entry): VWAP = the fair price of the day based on where most trading volume happened. The executor already enforces VWAP-aware entries in each phase — buying below or at VWAP gives you a better fill than the crowd. If the stock gaps up hard at open (above VWAP × 1.01 and more than 3% above entry), the Phase 1 check will skip it automatically to avoid a gap-up trap.
5
Close everything after 14 trading days (about 20 calendar days) — even if the trade hasn't hit TP or stop. This keeps your capital moving.
