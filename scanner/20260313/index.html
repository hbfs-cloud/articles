Methodology

1. Market Regime Detection

The regime detector uses 6 market components with equal weight: VIX (volatility), Credit (HYG/LQD spread), DXY (dollar strength), SPX (equity trend), TLT (bond momentum), and Liquidity (repo/Fed balance sheet). A composite score of 0.31/1.0 indicates deepened Risk-Off (below yesterday's 0.38). The regime deepened today on hot CPI (+3.1% YoY), VIX spike to 29.4, and bond sell-off to TLT $84.60. NLP analysis of 18 macro headlines confirms the dominant theme: stagflation.

2. Multi-Strategy Screening

Three DSL screeners run in parallel: Oversold Bounce (RSI<35 + volume>1.5x SMA20), Momentum Expansion (close>SMA20 + volume>2x + RSI 50-75), Breakout Squeeze (close>SMA50 + ATR14>ATR28×1.2). In Risk-Off deepened, strategy weights: Pre-Squeeze 40%, Breakout 35%, Oversold 15%, Momentum 10%. EU/Asia screeners were run but only EWD cleared the A+ threshold — all other EU/Asia candidates scored below 82. This confirms the retrospective lesson about selective use of geographic diversification.

3. Composite Scoring (4 Factors)

Each setup is scored 0-100: Technical (40%): RSI position, volume confirmation, moving average alignment, ATR momentum; Fundamental (25%): forward PE vs sector, revenue growth, dividend yield, balance sheet quality; Momentum (20%): price vs 20/50/200 DMA, relative sector performance, volume trend; Risk/Reward (15%): ATR-calibrated stop, target distance, historical win rate of pattern. Minimum score for inclusion: 85. Today's range: 85 (BITO) to 93 (CVX).

4. A+ Selection Criteria

A setup qualifies as A+ when: (1) Score ≥85/100; (2) Minimum 3 technical signals aligned (RSI, volume, S/R, pattern); (3) Identifiable catalyst (earnings, news, breakout technical); (4) Liquidity ≥$5M/day for stocks, ≥$50M/day for ETFs; (5) Market cap ≥$5B (Risk-Off filter); (6) Not in previous scan (anti-doublon filter); (7) Dilution check passed (no S-3 filings, no ATM offerings for small/mid caps).

5. Validation & Ranking

After scoring, a final validation pass checks: insider transactions (no significant selling in past 30 days), news sentiment (no hidden negative catalysts), SEC filings (no recent 8-K with material adverse events), and the retrospective feedback loop (avoiding tickers that were flops in recent retros).
