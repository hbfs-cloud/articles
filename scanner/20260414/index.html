Why RISK-ON despite the Iran Blockade?

The Iran Strait of Hormuz blockade is a geopolitical shock, not a structural economic shift. Markets have seen this playbook before: an oil spike, a brief VIX jump, then absorption as the situation either de-escalates or gets priced in. The key distinction is VIX behavior: Sunday night it briefly touched 22, but Monday’s session saw it retreat firmly below 20. This is the regime signal we track — not the intraday noise, but the closing behavior. Three consecutive sub-20 closes means institutional volatility sellers are dominant and the fear premium is being systematically removed. The Iran situation changes our sector allocation (more energy, more defense, less pure domestic consumer) but it does not flip the regime. RISK-ON with adjusted sector weights is the correct posture.

Each setup receives a score 0-100 based on: Technical (40%) — RSI position, MACD signal, SMA alignment, volume vs average; Momentum (30%) — 1-week, 1-month, 3-month price performance; Confluence (20%) — number of independent signals aligned (min 3 required for A+); Catalyst (10%) — identifiable near-term catalyst (earnings, sector rotation, macro event).
