Methodology

1. Market Regime Detection

The scanner analyzes 6 macro components (VIX, SPX, DXY, TLT, Credit, Liquidity) to determine one of 5 regimes: Risk-On, Neutral, Early Risk-Off, Risk-Off, or Recovery. Each component is scored 0–1 and the weighted average determines the regime. Today’s score of 0.40 places us in Early Risk-Off, where volatility is elevated (VIX = 0) but credit markets remain orderly (Credit = 1).

2. Multi-Strategy Screening

Four complementary strategies are deployed with regime-adjusted weights: Short Squeeze (40%), Pre-Squeeze (35%), Breakout Squeeze (15%), and Momentum Expansion (10%). In Early Risk-Off, squeeze strategies receive the highest weights because they exploit forced buying in defensive/commodity sectors under duress.

3. Composite Scoring (4 Factors)

Each candidate is scored on Technical (RSI, MACD, S/R, volume pattern), Fundamental (valuation, earnings quality, dividend), Momentum (price vs MAs, relative strength vs sector/index), and Risk (ATR sizing, stop distance, R/R ratio). The composite score ranges 0–100, with A+ setups requiring ≥85.

4. A+ Selection Criteria

Minimum score of 85/100, at least 3 confirming signals, R/R ≥ 1:1.5, sufficient liquidity (>$10M daily volume for stocks, >$50M for ETFs), sector diversification, and geographic diversification (min 5 US + 2 EU + 1 Asia + 2 ETFs). Dilution filter applied to all candidates below $5B market cap.

5. Validation & Ranking

Final candidates are cross-checked against retrospective feedback (4 retros, cumulative hit rates by strategy/sector), anti-duplicate filter (70%+ new tickers vs previous scan), and dilution/SEC checks. Rankings are by composite score descending.
