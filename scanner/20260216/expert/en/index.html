How to Read the RISK-OFF ROTATION Regime

In a Risk-Off Rotation phase, investors reposition from risky assets (tech, growth) to defensive stocks (healthcare, consumer staples, utilities, energy). This movement is triggered by rising volatility and a technical breakdown of the S&P 500. Gold in structural rally ($5040) confirms demand for safe-haven assets. The bearish dollar provides additional support to commodities and international stocks.
