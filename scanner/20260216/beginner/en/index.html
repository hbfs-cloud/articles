How to Read This Scanner

This scanner finds promising stock opportunities using simple indicators. Here's what everything means:

Key Indicators Explained Simply

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures if a stock is overbought or oversold on a scale of 0-100. Below 30 = oversold (stock might bounce up). Above 70 = overbought (stock might pull back). Between 30-70 = neutral.

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Shows momentum and trend direction. When MACD line crosses above signal line = bullish (buy signal). When it crosses below = bearish (sell signal). Also shows if momentum is accelerating or slowing down.

Support and Resistance

Support is a price level where buying pressure is strong (stock tends to bounce up from there). Resistance is a price level where selling pressure is strong (stock struggles to break above). When a stock breaks through resistance, it often moves quickly higher (breakout).

Volume

The number of shares traded. High volume on updays = strong buying (bullish). High volume on downdays = strong selling (bearish). Low volume = weak conviction. Volume confirms if a price move is real or fake.

Moving Averages

The average price over a period (like 20 days, 50 days, 200 days). When the stock is above its moving averages = uptrend. When below = downtrend. When moving averages are aligned (20 > 50 > 200), it confirms a strong trend.

Understanding the Star Ratings

  • ★★★★★ (5 stars): Excellent opportunity - all indicators aligned perfectly
  • ★★★★☆ (4 stars): Strong opportunity - most indicators positive
  • ★★★☆☆ (3 stars): Good opportunity - decent setup but some risk
  • ★★☆☆☆ (2 stars): Weak opportunity - mixed signals, higher risk
  • ★☆☆☆☆ (1 star): Poor opportunity - avoid

Understanding Risk/Reward Ratio

The Risk/Reward ratio shows how much you could gain compared to how much you could lose. For example:

  • 1:3.0: For every $1 you risk, you could make $3 (excellent)
  • 1:2.0: For every $1 you risk, you could make $2 (good)
  • 1:1.5: For every $1 you risk, you could make $1.50 (acceptable)
  • 1:1.0: Equal risk and reward (not recommended)

Look for setups with at least 1:2 risk/reward ratio. This means if you're right 50% of the time, you'll still make money overall.
