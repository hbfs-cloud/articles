🧠 Deep Dive: The Mechanics

1. Yen Carry Trade Unwinding

The "Carry Trade" involves borrowing cheap Yen (0.5%) to invest in higher-yielding assets like US Treasuries (4%) or Tech Stocks.

  • The Trigger: BoJ raised rates to 0.75%.
  • The Mechanism: Borrowing costs rise → Differential shrinks → Traders close positions.
  • The Risk: Estimated $3.66 Trillion unwinding could force selling of US assets.

2. Bitcoin Options "Gravitational Pull"

Why wait until Dec 27? Because of "Max Pain" theory on option expiry.

Before Dec 26 (Expiry)
Market Makers hedge positions, pinning price between large Call ($100k) and Put ($85k) walls. This suppresses volatility.
After Dec 26
Hedges are removed. The "gravitational pull" vanishes, allowing price to move freely. This is often when trends accelerate.
