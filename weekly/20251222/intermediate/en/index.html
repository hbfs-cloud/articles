🧠 3 Concepts to Master

Understanding these mechanics = making better decisions.

🇯🇵 1. Yen Carry Trade Unwind

For years, investors borrowed Yen at 0% to buy US stocks earning 10%+. Free money.

The problem: BoJ raises to 0.75%. Yen strengthens. Traders must close positions = forced selling.

🎯 Watch for: USD/JPY < 148=selling pressure on US stocks and Treasuries.

📊 2. Options Expiry = "Gravity"

Big players sell options. These options create "walls" that trap prices ($85k and $100k for BTC).

The mechanism: While options are "alive", price is prisoner. After expiry, walls disappear.

🎯 The trade: Wait AFTER December 26 to enter. The real move begins when the contract dies.

🪙 3. Debasement Trade

Governments print money → each bill is worth less. Gold and Bitcoin aren't rising, currency is falling.

The proof: Gold +60% YTD. Silver +130% YTD. Bitcoin -9% YTD. A notable divergence.

🎯 The implication: Having "hard assets" (Gold, BTC) protects your purchasing power. Minimum 10% of portfolio.
