🌊 Deep Dive: The "Yen Carry" Unwind

The Mechanism

Investors used to borrow Yen at near 0% to buy US Stocks (yielding 5%+). This is the "Carry Trade".

The Shift: The Bank of Japan (BoJ) is finally raising rates.
The Consequence: Borrowing Yen isn't cheap anymore. Investors are selling US assets to pay back their Yen loans. This creates selling pressure on the S&P 500 anytime the Yen strengthens.
