2. The Core: Fractal Dimension (Hurst)

We do not use moving averages (too slow). We measure the "roughness" of the price curve.

Trend (H > 0.6)
Momentum is persistent. We buy pullbacks.
Chaos (H ~ 0.5)
Random movement ("Brownian Motion"). We stay Cash.
Mean Reversion (H < 0.4)
Price will revert to mean. We short extensions.
