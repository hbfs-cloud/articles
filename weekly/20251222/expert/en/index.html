🎓 Pedagogical Deep Dive

Concept 1: Yen Carry Trade Unwinding

What is it?

The carry trade involves borrowing in a low-rate currency (Yen, 0.5%) to invest in higher-yielding assets (US Treasuries 4%, Equities). The total trade size is estimated at ~$3.66 Trillion.

Why is it reversing now?
  • The BoJ is finally hiking rates (0.5% → 0.75%).
  • The Yen is becoming expensive to borrow, narrowing the spread.
  • Traders are closing positions to pay back loans.
Impact: Forced Selling of US Equities + Yields Spike

Concept 2: Options Expiry "Gravitational Pull"

Mechanism

Market makers who sold options need to "hedge" their risk by buying or selling the underlying asset. This creates a magnetic force pulling the price toward "strike" prices with biggest Open Interest (OI).

Bitcoin Case (Dec 26)

Huge Put Wall at $85k and Call Wall at $100k. Price is likely to stay "pinned" between these until expiry.

Why does the move happen AFTER? Once options expire, market makers unwind their hedges. Gravity disappears, price is unleashed.

Concept 3: Debasement Trade (Gold + BTC)

Definition

Strategy to protect purchasing power against monetary expansion (M2). When money printing accelerates, fiat currency loses relative value.

  • Gold: Physical scarcity, 5000-year history.
  • Bitcoin: Algorithmic scarcity (21M Hard Cap).

This is why Gold is at ATH despite high real rates: market is pricing in future monetary inflation.
