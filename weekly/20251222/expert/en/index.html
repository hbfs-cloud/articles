🎓 Pedagogical Section (Deep Dive)

Concept 1: Yen Carry Trade Unwinding

What is it?
The carry trade consists of borrowing in a low-rate currency (Yen, 0.5%) to invest in higher-yielding assets (US Treasuries 4%, stocks).
Trade Size: ~$3.66 trillion of net Japanese position invested abroad.

Why is it reversing now?

  • BoJ hikes rates (0.5% → 0.75%)
  • Yen becomes more expensive to borrow
  • Rate differential narrows
  • Traders close their positions

Impact on US Markets:
Repatriation of Japanese capital → Selling of US Treasuries and stocks → Upward pressure on US yields.

Concept 2: Options Expiry "Gravitational Pull"

Mechanism:
Market makers who sell options must "hedge" their risk by buying/selling the underlying asset. This creates a force that pulls the price toward strikes with highest open interest.

Example Bitcoin Dec 26:

  • Put $85K with $1.4B open interest → Magnetic support
  • Call $100K with high concentration → Magnetic resistance
  • Price "stuck" between these two levels

Why the move comes AFTER expiry:
Once options expire, market makers no longer need to hedge. The "gravitational force" vanishes, and price can move freely.

Concept 3: Debasement Trade (Gold + Bitcoin)

Definition:
Investment strategy aiming to protect against purchasing power loss of fiat currencies due to monetary expansion (M2).

Assets involved:
Gold (Geological scarcity), Bitcoin (Algorithmic scarcity), Silver (Scarcity + industrial demand).

Mechanism:
When M2 increases → More money in circulation → Potential inflation → Gold and BTC appreciate as "stores of value".
