📚 2 Words to Remember

Get smarter in 1 minute.

"Debasement"

It's when your money loses value because there's too much of it.

🍹 Imagine: You dilute your juice with too much water → less flavor. Gold keeps the "flavor" (value).

"Expiry"

Options are bets with a deadline.

🎰 December 26: Big players fight to win their bet → prices move weirdly. Stay out.
