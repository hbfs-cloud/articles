AI: The engine for your savings. 🚀

Good news: AI-powered tech companies are still booming! But beware: the bank of Japan is shaking up the markets. We explain everything simply.

💡 The Essentials in 3 Key Points

Micron (AI) is booming!

The company making AI chips is selling everything it produces by "Micron". This reflects that AI is a solid investment for your portfolio!

Nike: China no longer buying

Nike's stock dropped 10% because Chinese consumers are spending less. Be cautious with luxury or auto brands heavily reliant on China.

Japan: Free money is over

The Bank of Japan raised its interest rates. In short: easy money is ending, which may make markets a bit nervous in January.

🌍 Japan, Simply Explained

Imagine you can borrow money at 0% in one country and invest it at 5% somewhere else. Many banks did this with Japan ("Carry Trade").

What does it mean for you?

When Japan raises its rates, this "trick" becomes less profitable. Investors bring their money back to Japan. This can cause market volatility (instability).

Our Advice: Keep 25% of your savings in cash (money available) to seize opportunities in January.

🛍️ Your Stocks: Where to put your money?

KEEP / BUY Tech & AI (ETFs)

AI-related companies are still the market engine. If you have Tech ETFs (baskets of stocks like S&P 500 or Nasdaq), hold onto them.

CAUTION Luxury & Auto (China)

Sales in China are weak. If a company relies heavily on China (like Nike or some luxury brands), it might suffer. Stay vigilant.

💎 Crypto: The Key Date for Bitcoin

Do nothing with Bitcoin before December 26th.

Dec 26: Special Day!

A huge amount of Bitcoin contracts expire that day. This can make the Bitcoin price very unstable.

Advice: Wait until December 27th to see if a clear trend emerges before investing.
