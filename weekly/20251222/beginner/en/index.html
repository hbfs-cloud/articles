Weekly Performance

🤔 Why is it moving? (Simply explained)

1. The end of "Free Money" in Japan

Imagine if you could borrow money for free in Japan to invest in the US. It was easy to win! But now, Japan is asking for interest. Investors are paying back debts, which shakes the markets a bit.

2. The Money Printer is On

Governments are printing a lot of money. When there are too many bills, each bill is worth less. That's why Gold and Bitcoin are going up: they keep their value when currency drops.

📝 Your Action Plan (Checklist)

  • Do nothing on Dec 26 morning. It's a "trap" date for Bitcoin (contract expiry). Wait for the afternoon.
  • Keep some Cash. About 25% of your money should be ready to invest if prices drop suddenly.
  • Bet on Defense. With global tensions, countries are spending more on security. It's a safe sector for 2026.

📚 Learning Minute

KEYWORD

"Debasement" (Devaluation)

It's when your money loses value because there is too much of it. Like diluting syrup with too much water. To keep the flavor (value), investors buy Gold.

KEYWORD

"Expiry" (Expiration)

Options are bets with a deadline. On the deadline day (expiry), big players fight to win their bets, which makes prices move weirdly.
