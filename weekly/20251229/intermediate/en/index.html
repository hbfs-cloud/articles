🎓 Educational Corner: The "Stop Loss"

Why use a Stop Loss?

In our analyses (like for $EXAS), we always indicate a "Stop Loss" level. This is your seatbelt. If the price falls below this level, it means our bullish scenario is invalid. It is better to sell and cut losses quickly rather than hoping for a rebound that may never come.

Golden Rule: Cut losses early, let winners run.
