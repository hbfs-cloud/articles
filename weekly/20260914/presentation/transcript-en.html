Shared Risk: The Correlation Matrix

This matrix measures how daily returns moved together over the stated common window. A value near plus one means stronger co-movement in that sample; a value near zero means little linear relationship there.

Holding Through the Fed: Decide Before Entry

Scene 19

Index Announcement ≠ Guaranteed Imbalance

An index announcement begins a chain of portfolio adjustments. Funds following the affected benchmarks may have to buy or sell, but the final market impact depends on weights, assets, existing positioning, and how execution is distributed through time.

Other traders can anticipate those flows. Opposing index changes can offset part of them. That is why we do not translate a confirmed addition into a guaranteed closing rally.

Our intraday plan still requires an exit at nine fifty in the evening Paris time.

Scene 20

Continuation: Freeze the Base Before the Signal

After price crosses the pivot, wait for two completed, consecutive five-minute candles. Their ranges must overlap and both closes must be above the pivot. Freeze the base high as B, the base low as L, and width W as B minus L.

The original stop is L minus two actual exchange ticks. A strictly later five-minute candle must close above B. The two candles that define the base cannot also be the confirmation candle.

Freezing the structure prevents the entry rule from moving with a rising quote. Monday’s tick size must come from the instrument, not an assumption that every share trades in one-cent increments.

Scene 21

The Cap Protects the Available R-Multiple

The first purchase ceiling is B plus one quarter of W, rounded down to the actual tick. When known resistance exists, first target T1 is one tick below it. Let S be the original stop.

The reward constraint gives a second ceiling: T1 plus one-point-five times S, divided by two-point-five. The permitted cap is the lower of these two ceilings. It must still be at least B plus one tick.

If that fails, the base cannot support a purchase. Confirmation can occur above the ceiling; it is the limit price, not the confirmation close, that stays capped.

Scene 22

One IOC Attempt Within Five Minutes

After confirmation, the execution window is one five-minute interval. The ask must be strictly above B and at or below the ceiling. The last traded price must be at least one tick above B. Then make one limit IOC attempt only.

Abort before purchase if either ask or last returns to B or below, if the structural stop is touched, if the window expires, or if fixed T1 is reached before the buy. An unfilled IOC is not retried.

Any restart requires a new, complete base. The protocol records an unavailable setup rather than turning a late quote into a chase.

Scene 23

Pullbacks: Three Fallback Cards

This table collects the backup numbers in one place for the presentation. The trigger is not simply a print at the recovery level. A fifteen-minute candle must touch the specified lower zone, and a strictly later fifteen-minute candle must close above C.

Only then does the five-minute execution window begin. The ask must be above C and within the maximum purchase price, while the last price must be at least one tick above C.

Scene 24

R Outcomes Describe Position Management

Position management starts from the average price actually paid. R is that average entry minus the original stop. Protect the quantity actually filled immediately. At the first target, sell half, rounded down to a whole share with a minimum of one.

Only after that entire tranche is effectively sold do we move the stop on the remainder to the average entry. Sell the balance at the second target.

Scene 25

Time Is Part of Risk

Time is part of the setup. US entries may begin at 13:45 GMT, 15:45 Paris; Europe may begin at 07:15 GMT, 09:15 Paris, once the required structure exists.

Normal entry cutoffs are 18:00 GMT, 20:00 Paris for the US and 14:00 GMT, 16:00 Paris for Europe.

Intraday positions close by 19:50 GMT, 21:50 Paris in the US, and 15:20 GMT, 17:20 Paris in Europe, subject to the earlier Fed NO deadline.

After sixty minutes, exit a position that has never reached plus one-half R and remains at or below entry. A losing intraday position is never relabelled a swing merely to defer a decision.
